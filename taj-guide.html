😰 The "Outstanding Ticket" Panic

We've all been there: You show up to renew your license, feeling confident, only to be told the system is "locked" because of a $500 ticket from 2019 that you completely forgot about.

Now you have to leave, go to Court (which is packed), and lose a whole day of work. Stop. Do not leave your house until you read this.

🚓 Step 1: The Ticket Lookup

Before you even think about renewing, you must check your status. The system is linked. If you owe money, the machine will reject you.

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING If you have a warrant or unpaid ticket, do not go to the Tax Office. You will be turned away.

How to Check (Secret Weapon)

You don't need to visit the court yet. Use the online portal to check your status instantly.

📱 Enter Details: Use your TRN or Driver's License Number to search.
💳 Pay Online: If the ticket is visible and NOT marked "Court Required," pay it immediately via the TAJ Portal using a credit/debit card.
🏛️ Court Date Passed? If the ticket says "Warrant" or "Court," you cannot pay online. You must visit the specific Parish Court listed on the ticket to clear it.

🪪 Step 2: License Renewal

If your tickets are clear, you have the "Green Light." Do not waste time in the general line.

The "One-Trip" Protocol

✅ Form F7: Download and fill this out before you leave home. Don't be the person borrowing a pen at the counter.
✅ Photos: If your license photo is over 5 years old, bring 1 passport-sized photo (certified by a JP).
✅ The Fees (2026):
• Private/General (5 Years): $9,240 JMD
• Motorcycle (5 Years): $3,690 JMD
• Lost License Replacement: ~$4,500 JMD

🚗 Step 3: Motor Vehicle (Registration)

Good News: The "stickers" on your windshield are gone. Registration is now 100% digital (eMVRC).

💡 Pro Tip: You do NOT need to visit the tax office for this.

The "Sofa" Renewal

💻 Log in to TAJ: Ensure you have a valid Insurance Certificate and Fitness already in the system.
📧 Check Email: Once paid, the Digital Registration Certificate (eMVRC) is emailed to you.
👮 Police Check: Traffic cops now use tablets to scan your plate. You are legal instantly. Keep the email screenshot just in case.
