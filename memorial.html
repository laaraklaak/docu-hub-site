Traditional funeral programs are limited by page count and eventually fade away. We design premium Smart Memorial Keepsakes that unlock an infinite, permanent digital tribute with a single tap.

Beyond the Booklet.

Funeral homes provide standard paper booklets. Docu-Hub provides a modern, dignified legacy. Our Smart Keepsakes fit perfectly in a wallet, ensuring family and friends can carry the memory of their loved one with them forever.

The Physical Keepsake

We craft a beautiful, high-quality Matte Black PVC card featuring elegant typography and subtle gold accents. It is a physical token of remembrance designed to last a lifetime, presented in a custom security sleeve.

  • Dignified Design: Minimalist, respectful, and completely customized for your family.
  • Built to Last: Made from durable, waterproof materials—not fragile paper.
  • Embedded Technology: Hidden inside the card is a secure NFC microchip.

The Digital Tribute

When friends and family tap the Keepsake Card with their smartphone, it instantly opens a beautifully formatted Digital Tribute. No app downloads are required. Just a seamless celebration of life.

  • Infinite Photo Gallery: Never worry about the cost of printing extra pages. Share hundreds of photos.
  • Video Integration: Include a video montage, favorite songs, or recorded speeches.
  • The Full Story: Room for the complete obituary, family acknowledgments, and order of service.
