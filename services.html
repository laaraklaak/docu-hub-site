1. Passport Renewal & Name Change (PICA)

PICA Agency Proxy

Qualification & Prerequisites

  • Eligibility: Adult Jamaican passport holders renewing an expired/expiring passport or applying for a legal name update.
  • Photos: Two (2) identical passport photos meeting strict PICA specs (white background, no glare, unmounted).
  • Legal Name Change: Original certified Marriage Certificate or Deed Poll (if updating name from current passport).
  • Primary ID & TRN: Valid government-issued photo ID and Jamaican Tax Registration Number.

What Docu-Hub Handles

  • Pre-Submission Audit: Complete verification of photo dimensions, legal spelling, and document certification.
  • Portal Execution: Pre-filling and lodging all official electronic PICA filings to prevent portal errors.
  • Physical Proxy Submission: In-person delivery of original notarized documents at PICA Kingston for legal updates.
  • Tracked Dispatch: Continuous tracking and secure doorstep delivery of your finalized passport.
Processing starts at $145 USD

2. Motor Vehicle License Renewal (TAJ)

TAJ Licensing Proxy

Qualification & Prerequisites

  • Eligibility: Registered owners of private motor vehicles requiring road tax renewal via Tax Administration Jamaica.
  • Fitness Certificate: Active, valid Fitness Certificate recorded in the TAJ database.
  • Insurance Cover Note: Active Jamaican motor vehicle insurance policy covering the renewal period.
  • Vehicle Details: Title number, registration number, and owner's TRN.

What Docu-Hub Handles

  • Compliance Verification: Pre-checking fitness and insurance alignment in the database before submitting payment.
  • Tax Processing: Direct payment processing of official 1-year or 6-month TAJ road tax fees.
  • Digital eMVRC Dispatch: Transmitting your official electronic Motor Vehicle Registration Certificate via email upon clearance.
Flat Processing Fee: $85 USD

3. Vital Records Search & Retrieval (RGD)

RGD Search Suite

Qualification & Prerequisites

  • Eligibility: Individuals seeking official certified copies of Jamaican birth, marriage, or death certificates.
  • Key Details: Full name at birth/event, approximate date of event, parish, and parents' full names.
  • Authorization: Signed authorization form if requesting records on behalf of a family member.

What Docu-Hub Handles

  • Registry Search: Conducting online and manual archival ledger lookups at the Registrar General's Department.
  • Application Monitoring: Following up directly with RGD officers to resolve entry mismatches or spelling discrepancies.
  • Sealed Courier Delivery: Securing clean, official prints and dispatching via signature-required international shipping.
Processing starts at $115 USD

4. NIS Verification & Form Guidance

NIS Guidance Proxy

Qualification & Prerequisites

  • Eligibility: Jamaican workers local or abroad tracking contribution records, voluntary status, or preparing benefit claims.
  • Information Required: Full legal name, NIS number (if known), TRN, date of birth, and employer history details.
  • Benefit Claims: Depending on the claim type (e.g., Funeral Grants), official certificates and relationship proof are required.

What Docu-Hub Handles

  • Record Retrieval: Liaising with NIS offices to pull and verify official contribution history statements.
  • Voluntary Setup: Structuring required documentation for overseas Jamaicans seeking to maintain active benefit status.
  • Form Preparation: Meticulous prep and pre-auditing of official NIS claim forms to prevent agency rejection.
Processing starts at $165 USD
