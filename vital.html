Vital Records Search & Retrieval

Before we can conduct a deep archival search or correct an error on your ledger, we need specific historical information. Review the exact details you need to provide for each RGD service.

Birth Certificate Retrieval

Secure fresh, certified copies of an existing birth record for immigration, passport renewals, or personal files. We handle both digital retrievals and manual archival ledger searches for older records.

Required Information

  • Subject’s exact full name at birth
  • Exact or approximate Date of Birth
  • Parish and specific location of birth
  • Mother’s full maiden name
  • Father’s full name (if legally on record)

The Search Workflow

  • We cross-reference your details to ensure an accurate query.
  • If not digital, our agents request a manual archival search.
  • Minor spelling discrepancies are resolved by us locally.
  • Certified prints are dispatched internationally via courier.

Marriage Certificate Retrieval

Retrieve official, certified copies of marriage records registered anywhere in Jamaica for spousal sponsorship, name changes, or legal updates in foreign jurisdictions.

Required Information

  • Groom’s full name
  • Bride’s full maiden name
  • Exact Date of Marriage
  • Parish and exact location (Church, etc.)

The Search Workflow

  • Submit the couple's details through our secure portal.
  • Our team submits the targeted search request to the RGD.
  • We follow up on delayed registrations from marriage officers.
  • The certified marriage copy is retrieved and couriered globally.

Death Certificate Retrieval

Secure official death records required for settling estates overseas, processing NIS funeral grants, or claiming international life insurance policies.

Required Information

  • Full name of the deceased
  • Exact Date of Death
  • Parish and specific place of death
  • Age of the subject at time of death

The Search Workflow

  • We initiate a priority search using specific identifiers.
  • If RGD requires more details, we act as your local liaison.
  • We secure the certified pink copies required for legal matters.
  • Records are securely sealed and dispatched via courier.

Record Corrections & Updates

If a record contains spelling errors, is missing a father's name, or was never officially registered (Late Registration), the original ledger must be legally amended before a valid certificate can be printed.

Legal Requirements

  • Signed statutory declarations from parents
  • Valid government photo IDs for declarants
  • Supporting documentary evidence
  • Overseas signatures must be Notarized

The Amendment Workflow

  • We draft the exact statutory declarations required.
  • You print, notarize, and courier the declarations to Jamaica.
  • Our proxy submits the case and tracks it through the RGD.
  • A fresh, corrected certificate is printed and shipped.

Universal Core Requirements for Proxy

Valid Government ID

The person requesting the record must upload a copy of their valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License) to initiate the search.

Signed Authorization Form

Due to RGD privacy regulations, if you are requesting records on behalf of a family member, you must sign a formal Docu-Hub proxy authorization letter.
