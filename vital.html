Jamaican Vital Records

Before booking a consultation for Birth, Marriage, or Death certificates, please review the strict rules below regarding entry numbers, archival searches, and privacy constraints.

Do you have your Entry Number?

If you have an old copy of the certificate with the Entry Number (or the pink marriage slip), you qualify for the Fast Track application. If you do NOT have the Entry Number, a mandatory Archival Search must be conducted before a certificate can be requested.

Applicant vs. Subject Rule

RGD strictly enforces privacy laws. If you are not the person named on the certificate, you must provide your own valid ID, contact details, and a clear declaration of your exact relationship to the subject.

Standard Certificate Requirements

Whether you have the Entry Number or need us to conduct a search first, we must have the exact details below to initiate the RGD workflow:

  • Birth Certificates You must provide the exact Date of Birth, the Parish of Birth, and the Mother’s full maiden name at the time of birth.
  • Marriage Certificates You must provide the exact Date of Marriage, the specific Church/Venue and Parish, the Groom’s full name, the Bride’s maiden name, and ideally the Marriage Officer’s name.
  • Death Certificates You must provide the exact Date and Place of Death, the deceased’s full legal name, and the name of the person who registered the death (the informant).

Legal Updates & Proxy Pickup

Updates to vital records are strict legal proceedings. Review the realities of these complex scenarios below.

Correction of Error

  • Old Official Evidence: You must provide at least two pieces of older official evidence showing the correct info (e.g., old clinic book, early school records).
  • Independent Witness: A declaration from someone who is at least 10 years older than you and has known you since birth.
  • All photocopies must be certified by a Justice of the Peace (JP).

Adding a Father’s Name (Status)

  • Requires the child’s Birth Entry Number and valid IDs for the parents.
  • Physical Signatures: Parents must physically sign the 3-page PATAC form in the presence of a JP, Attorney, or Notary Public.
  • Note: If the mother is deceased, an original Death Certificate is required in place of her signature, and the father will sign the form as he normally would. (If the father is deceased, a Family Court Declaration of Paternity is required).

Proxy Pickup Authorization Warning

RGD is extremely strict with third-party collections. If Docu-Hub is picking up a physical certificate on your behalf, you must sign a Letter of Authorization. Your signature on that letter MUST perfectly match the signature on the valid ID you submit to us. If the signatures do not match, RGD will reject the proxy pickup.
