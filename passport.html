Jamaican Passport Services

Different passport scenarios require completely different workflows. Review the exact required documents below to see if you are ready to proceed with an online renewal or a physical proxy walk-in.

Standard Online Renewal (Adults)

Streamlined processing for adult passport renewals where there are no legal name changes, and you currently possess your undamaged, expiring or expired passport book.

Requirements & References

  • Applicant must be 18 years or older
  • Physical passport book in hand (undamaged)
  • Compliant digital photo meeting PICA specs
  • Official Reference details (JP, Minister, etc.)

Processing & Delivery Speeds

  • We audit your digital photo and credentials.
  • Application submitted directly into the PICA portal.
  • Standard Processing (14 days) or Express local turnaround.
  • Dispatched via international courier (DHL) or local pickup.

First-Time Adult (Transitioning at 18+)

When a minor reaches age 18, their application is legally treated as a brand-new adult passport. It cannot be renewed through the portal and requires a physical walk-in proxy submission.

Required Documents

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Valid Government Photo ID (e.g. State ID)
  • Two printed PICA-compliant passport photos
  • Official PICA Form signed by applicant in person

The Walk-In Proxy Workflow

  • The 18+ applicant completes and signs the forms.
  • Courier the wet-signed forms and photos to our Jamaica office.
  • Our proxy agents queue in person at PICA to lodge the application.
  • Your new 10-year passport is retrieved and shipped globally.

Minor Applications (Under 18)

Applications for children under age 18 must be initiated by a parent or legal guardian. Strict legal safeguards require parent authorization and specific identification proof.

Guardian Requirements

  • Applying Parent's valid Government Photo ID
  • Minor's Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Official Court Guardianship Orders (if applicable)
  • Sections C & E signed by parent

Consent Rules

  • If parent is overseas, a notarized Consent Letter is required.
  • We audit all minor forms to prevent PICA consent rejections.
  • Our proxies present the documentation at the PICA desk.
  • The child's 5-year passport is retrieved and dispatched.

Legal Name Changes

Updating your passport name following Marriage, Divorce, or an official Deed Poll requires physical surrender of supporting legal documents and an in-person proxy submission.

Document Proof Required

  • Original Certified Marriage Certificate
  • Original Absolute Divorce Decree
  • Registered RGD Deed Poll (if applicable)
  • Current Passport Book to be replaced

The Walk-In Workflow

  • We review your legal document chain for PICA compliance.
  • Courier your wet-signed application and original certificates.
  • Our proxy agent presents the legal proof to a PICA officer.
  • Your updated passport is issued and delivered back to you.

Damaged Passports

If your passport was washed, torn, defaced, or has an unreadable biometric data page, it is legally invalid. Damaged books cannot be renewed online and must be surrendered in person.

Mandatory Requirements

  • Physical surrender of the damaged passport
  • Sworn, Notarized Affidavit explaining damage
  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Valid alternative Government Photo ID

The Evaluation Workflow

  • We draft the formal damage explanation affidavit.
  • You courier the physical damaged book and notarized packet.
  • Our proxy delivers the book for official evaluation at PICA.
  • Your replacement passport is processed and dispatched.

Lost or Stolen Passports

Replacing a lost or stolen passport from overseas involves strict identity security measures to prevent fraud, including mandatory police reports and official agency investigations.

Mandatory Documentation

  • Official Police Report of loss
  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Alternative valid Government Photo ID
  • Specialized PICA Lost Passport Form

The Security Workflow

  • We review your police report and identity documents.
  • You courier original signed forms and police report.
  • Our proxy submits the packet directly to PICA investigators.
  • Once cleared, your replacement passport is issued.
