Physical Passport Required All Cases
A digital picture of your bio-page is NOT sufficient. Unless filing for an official "Lost Passport," you must have your physical passport book in hand (even if it is severely damaged).
The Signature Box Rule In-Person & Proxy Only
For physical form submissions, your signature must sit completely inside the designated box. If the ink touches or crosses the border lines, or if signed in any color other than blue or black ink, PICA will reject the application outright. (Does not apply to standard online renewals).
No Laminated or Altered Documents All Cases
PICA strictly rejects civil documents that have been laminated, taped, or altered. Your original RGD Birth Certificate and Marriage Certificates must be unadulterated physical originals.
Proxy Authorization Protocol Proxy Filings Only
We pre-audit your file using digital scans. However, acting as your official administrative proxy in Jamaica means you will eventually need to provide us with an Authorization Letter and a copy of your valid ID—both certified by a Notary Public or Justice of the Peace (JP).

Standard Adult Renewal

Online

For direct renewals only (no name changes). Current passport must be physically in hand and undamaged.

  • Must be 18 years or older
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Current passport issued after September 2001
  • PICA-Standard Digital Photo: Electronic digital file meeting strict lighting/sizing checkers (not a photo of a printed photo)
  • Two (2) Contact References: Full names, physical addresses, phone numbers, and emails of two people who do not live with you.

First-Time Adult (18+)

In Person / Proxy

Requires physical submission via our proxy. This cannot be filed online.

Damaged Passport

Replacement

Requires physical submission via our proxy. Do not discard the damaged book.

  • The Damaged Book: You must physically present the book (even if mutilated or torn).
  • Notarized Damage Statement: Written declaration detailing how the damage occurred. (We supply a strict PICA-approved template to prevent rejection).
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Two (2) Printed Passport Photos: One photo certified by an Authorized Official (JP/Minister).
  • Certified Form & Valid ID: Form Section G certified by an Authorized Official, plus a valid photo ID.
