Jamaican Passport Services

Explore the requirements for standard online renewals, complex name changes, and replacing lost or damaged documents from overseas.

Determine Your Pathway

Which process applies to you?

Standard Online Renewal

You qualify for the streamlined online portal processing if ALL of the following apply to you:

  • Adult Applicant: You are 18 years or older.
  • Exact Same Name: You are not making any legal name changes.
  • Physical Book in Hand: Your current/expired passport is in your possession.
  • Not Damaged: The book is not water-logged, torn, or defaced.
Name Changes & Minors

You require our physical walk-in proxy service if you fall into these categories:

  • Minors & Transitions: Applying for a minor, or transitioning a minor's passport to an adult passport.
  • Marriage/Divorce: Updating your name using a Marriage Certificate or Divorce Decree.
  • Deed Poll: Updating your name via an official RGD Deed Poll document.
Lost or Stolen Passport

If your passport is completely lost or stolen overseas, PICA requires strict protocols to protect your identity:

  • Police Report: You must immediately obtain an official report from your local precinct.
  • ID Verification: You must provide valid government photo ID (Driver's License, State ID).
  • Specialized Form: The PICA application must detail exactly how the book was lost.
  • Higher Fees: Replacing a lost/stolen book carries a penalty fee.
Damaged Passport

If your passport was washed, torn, or the data page is unreadable, it is invalid for travel. You will need:

  • Surrender the Book: You MUST keep and physically submit the damaged passport.
  • Sworn Affidavit: A signed, notarized statement explaining exactly how the damage occurred.
  • Proxy Walk-in: Damaged passports cannot be renewed online; they must be evaluated in person.

Required For All Applications

Proof of Citizenship

Your Original Jamaican Birth Certificate, Adoption Certificate, or Citizenship Certificate. Required to prove identity.

Two Passport Photos

Photos must meet exact PICA specs: crisp white background, no glare, unmounted, and taken within 6 months.
