Standard Online Renewal (Adults)

Streamlined processing for adult passport renewals where there are no legal name changes, and you currently possess your undamaged, expiring or expired passport book.

Requirements & References

  • Applicant must be 18 years or older
  • Physical passport book in hand (undamaged)
  • Compliant digital photo meeting PICA specs
  • Official Reference details (JP, Minister of Religion, or Doctor)

Processing & Delivery Speeds

  • We audit your digital photo and reference credentials for compliance.
  • Application submitted directly into the PICA online portal.
  • Choose between Standard Processing (14 days) or Express local turnaround (varies).
  • Dispatched via international signature-required courier (DHL) or local pickup.

First-Time Adult (Transitioning at 18+)

When a minor reaches age 18, their application is legally treated as a brand-new adult passport. It cannot be renewed through the online portal and requires a physical walk-in proxy submission at PICA.

Required Documents

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate / Citizenship Certificate
  • Applicant's valid Government Photo ID (Driver's License / State ID)
  • Two printed PICA-compliant passport photos (one certified by a JP)
  • Official PICA Application Form signed by the applicant in person

The Walk-In Proxy Workflow

  • The 18+ applicant completes and signs their own application forms.
  • You courier the wet-signed original forms and photos to our Jamaica office.
  • Our proxy agents queue in person at PICA headquarters to lodge the application.
  • Your new 10-year adult passport is retrieved and shipped globally via courier.

Minor Applications (Under 18)

Applications for children under age 18 must be initiated by a parent or legal guardian. Strict legal safeguards require parent authorization and specific identification proof.

Guardian Requirements

  • Applying Parent/Guardian's valid Government Photo ID
  • Minor's Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Official Court Guardianship Orders (if applicant is not the natural parent)
  • Completed Section C & E on PICA form signed by parent

Consent & Overseas Proxy Rules

  • If parent is overseas, a notarized Consent Letter & notarized ID copy are required.
  • Docu-Hub audits all minor forms to prevent PICA consent rejections.
  • Our proxy agents present the physical documentation packet at the PICA desk.
  • The child's 5-year passport is retrieved and dispatched to your international address.

Legal Name Changes

Updating your passport name following Marriage, Divorce, or an official Deed Poll requires physical surrender of supporting legal documents and an in-person proxy submission.

Document Proof Required

  • Original Certified Marriage Certificate (if updating due to marriage)
  • Original Absolute Divorce Decree & Birth Certificate (if reverting to maiden name)
  • Registered RGD Deed Poll (if name changed legally)
  • Current Passport Book to be replaced

The Proxy Walk-In Workflow

  • We review your legal document chain to ensure strict PICA compliance.
  • You courier your wet-signed application and original legal certificates to Jamaica.
  • Our proxy agent presents the legal proof directly to a PICA officer.
  • Your updated passport is issued and delivered back to you via international courier.

Damaged Passports

If your passport was washed, torn, defaced, or has an unreadable biometric data page, it is legally invalid. Damaged books cannot be renewed online and must be surrendered in person.

Mandatory Requirements

  • Physical surrender of the damaged passport book
  • Sworn, Notarized Affidavit explaining exact cause of damage
  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate or Citizenship document
  • Valid alternative Government Photo ID

The Evaluation Workflow

  • We draft the formal damage explanation affidavit for your notary signature.
  • You courier the physical damaged book and notarized packet to Jamaica.
  • Our proxy delivers the book for official evaluation at PICA headquarters.
  • Your replacement passport is processed and dispatched to your door.

Lost or Stolen Passports

Replacing a lost or stolen passport from overseas involves strict identity security measures to prevent fraud, including mandatory police reports and official agency investigations.

Mandatory Documentation

  • Official Police Report from local precinct where loss occurred
  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Alternative valid Government Photo ID
  • Specialized PICA Lost Passport Form detailing circumstances

The Security Workflow

  • We review your police report and identity documents for PICA acceptance.
  • You courier original signed forms and police report to our Jamaica office.
  • Our proxy agent submits the lost report packet directly to PICA investigators.
  • Once cleared, your replacement passport is issued and delivered via courier.

Universal Core Requirements

Proof of Jamaican Citizenship

An Original Jamaican Birth Certificate, Adoption Certificate, or Citizenship Certificate is strictly required for all physical proxy walk-ins.

Two Compliant Passport Photos

Photos must adhere strictly to PICA specs: crisp white background, no glare, unmounted, taken within 6 months, and certified on the reverse by a JP where required.

Service Advisory

Navigating PICA Requirements Overseas

For Jamaicans living in the diaspora, dealing with expired, lost, or damaged travel documents can create immense anxiety—especially when travel plans or visa statuses depend on a valid passport.

While standard adult renewals can be completed online, complex situations such as legal name changes, minor applications, 18+ adult transitions, and damaged books require physical walk-in representation at PICA headquarters in Kingston.

"Incomplete forms, unverified references, or missing consent signatures are the leading causes of PICA application rejections."

The Solution: Docu-Hub Operations serves as your secure on-the-ground proxy desk. We pre-audit every document, reference credential, and photo prior to submission, then physically walk your application through the PICA desks to ensure seamless processing.
