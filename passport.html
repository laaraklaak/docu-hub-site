Jamaican Passport Services

Before booking a consultation, please review the strict eligibility rules and document checklists below to ensure your application will be accepted by PICA without delays.

Physical Passport Required

A digital picture of your bio-page is NOT sufficient. Unless filing for a "Lost Passport", you must have your physical passport book in hand (even if it is damaged).

The Proxy Reality Check

While we take digital copies initially to pre-audit your file, acting as your official proxy in Jamaica means we will eventually need the original physical documents and wet signatures.

The "Often Forgotten" Checklist

Most clients assume they only need their old passport to renew. Before contacting us, ensure you have the following critical items ready:

  • Your Original Birth Certificate Even for a standard renewal, PICA frequently requires verification of your original RGD birth certificate. Do not assume your old passport is enough.
  • PICA-Standard Passport Photos For online renewals, a professional digital/electronic file is required (taking a picture of a printed photo will fail the checker). For all other physical applications, you must provide two (2) printed passport-sized photos. All photos must meet strict PICA lighting and dimension rules.
  • Two (2) Contact References You do not need signed character forms. However, you must provide the full names, physical addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses of two people who do not live with you. This is a mandatory requirement for every application type.

Choose Your Scenario

Locate your specific situation below to see exactly what documents you need to gather before we begin processing your file.

1. Standard Adult Renewal (Online)

  • Must be 18 years or older.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Current passport must be issued after 2001.
  • Passport must be physically in hand and undamaged.
  • PICA-Standard Digital Photo (electronic file).
  • Direct renewals only (no name changes).
  • Details for two (2) contact references (not living with you).

2. First-Time Adult (18+)

  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Valid Government ID: Voter's ID or Driver's License strictly required (school IDs are not accepted).
  • Two (2) printed passport-sized photos.
  • Details for two (2) contact references (not living with you).
  • Requires physical submission via proxy.

3. Name Change (Marriage/Divorce)

  • Current physical passport (no additional ID required).
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Certified copy of the Marriage Certificate OR Decree Absolute (divorce papers).
  • Two (2) printed passport-sized photos.
  • Details for two (2) contact references (not living with you).
  • Requires physical submission via proxy (cannot be done online).

4. Damaged Passport Protocol

  • The Damaged Passport: You must physically present the damaged book (do not throw it away).
  • Notarized Report: A written statement explaining how the damage occurred. (Note: We provide a strict PICA-approved template for this, ensuring the wording, signature, and Notary/JP stamps are formatted perfectly to avoid rejection.)
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Two (2) printed passport-sized photos.
  • Details for two (2) contact references (not living with you).
  • Requires physical submission via proxy.

5. Lost Passport Protocol

  • Original Police Report: Must be signed and stamped.
  • Valid government-issued photo ID.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Two (2) printed passport-sized photos.
  • Details for two (2) contact references (not living with you).
  • Note: Do not panic about the police station. It is a routine administrative step. Make the report, get the physical copy, and we will handle the rest.

Official PICA Fees & Expedited Options

PICA offers standard and expedited processing times. Please note that replacing a lost or damaged passport carries a mandatory government penalty fee.

Application Type Standard (7-14 Days) Expedited (3 Days) Next Day (24 Hrs)
Renewal / First-Time Adult $6,500 JMD $9,500 JMD $11,500 JMD
Lost / Damaged Replacement $16,500 JMD $19,500 JMD $21,500 JMD

*Note: Docu-Hub Operations charges a separate flat-fee for our proxy and pre-audit services.

Mandatory Direct Courier Delivery

To ensure the absolute highest level of security for your travel documents, Docu-Hub Operations does not collect or hold finished physical passports on your behalf. Once your passport is printed, it must be dispatched directly from PICA to your doorstep via secure courier (such as Tara Couriers locally or DHL internationally). Courier fees are assessed separately based on your specific delivery destination.
