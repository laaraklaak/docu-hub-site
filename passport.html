Standard Adult Renewal

For direct renewals only (no name changes). Current passport must be physically in hand and undamaged.

  • Must be 18 years or older
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Current passport issued after September 2001
  • PICA-Standard Photo: Physical printed photos (if doing in-person proxy) OR professional digital file (if using online portal).
  • Two (2) Contact References: Full names, physical addresses, phone numbers, and emails of two people who do not live with you.
✈️ Attention Overseas Applicants:

Because you are overseas, you cannot use our physical proxy service. However, we provide complete online guidance and a strict checklist to ensure zero rejections through the official online portal. Note: You must obtain a professional digital passport photo; we do not format photos.

First-Time Adult (18+)

This cannot be filed online. It requires physical submission via our local proxy or through an overseas Consulate.

  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Valid Government ID: Voter's ID, Driver's License, or National ID (School IDs are strictly rejected)
  • Two (2) Printed Passport Photos: One photo must be officially certified on the back by an Authorized Official (JP, Minister, or Police Officer).
  • Certified Application Form: Section G of the physical form must be signed and stamped by the same certifying official.

Mandatory Standards

General Filing Rules

Ensure your documents meet these strict PICA standards before booking.

Physical Passport Required All Cases
A digital picture of your bio-page is NOT sufficient. Unless filing for an official "Lost Passport," you must have your physical passport book in hand (even if it is severely damaged).
The Signature Box Rule In-Person & Consulates
For physical form submissions, your signature must sit completely inside the designated box. If the ink touches or crosses the border lines, or if signed in any color other than blue or black ink, PICA will reject the application outright. (Does not apply to standard online renewals).
No Laminated or Altered Documents All Cases
PICA strictly rejects civil documents that have been laminated, taped, or altered. Your original RGD Birth Certificate and Marriage Certificates must be unadulterated physical originals.
