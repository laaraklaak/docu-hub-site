Search Requirements

To successfully find a record, the RGD database requires exact matches. Please have the following ready:

  • Correct Spelling: Names must match the original register.
  • Parish of Event: Where the birth/death physically occurred.
  • Date of Event: Year is critical for the search index.
  • Entry Number: (Optional) Helps speed up the process.

Critical Policy

RGD Search Fees are non-refundable. If a record cannot be found due to incorrect information provided (e.g., wrong parish or year), the search fee is retained by the agency. Please double-check your dates.
