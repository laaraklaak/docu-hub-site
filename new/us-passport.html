UK Adult Passport Renewal

Renewing your British passport from Jamaica shouldn't be a gamble. We handle the complex HMPO digital requirements, photo compliance, and secure courier logistics so you can travel without fear of rejection.

How We Handle It

1. Pre-Check

We review your old passport and documents to identify any name changes or damage issues before we begin.

2. Digital Compliance

We take and validate your photo against HMPO biometric standards and secure your countersignatory if required.

3. Secure Logistics

We ship your originals via tracked courier and monitor the application until the new passport lands in your hand.

Requirements for Success

Primary Documents

  • Current UK Passport: Must be the original booklet.
  • Digital Photo Code: We generate this in-office.
  • Other Passports: Any uncancelled Jamaican passports.

Support & Eligibility

  • Marriage Certificate: Required if name changed.
  • Proof of Address: Recent utility bill.
  • Color of Shirt: Plain grey or dark shirt only.
  • No Glasses: Must be removed for photo.
