US Adult Passport Renewal

Renew your US Passport without the hassle of bank drafts or complicated forms. We handle the Pay.gov online payment, DS-82 form prep, photo compliance, and DHL logistics directly to the Embassy.

How We Handle It

1. Eligibility

We confirm your passport was issued less than 15 years ago and is not damaged (Standard DS-82 Renewal).

2. Secure Payment

We process the $130 USD government fee securely via Pay.gov so you don't need a bank draft.

3. Embassy Courier

We handle the DHL waybills to ship your application to the US Embassy in Kingston and track its return.

Requirements for Success

Primary Documents

  • Current US Passport: Must be submitted (returned to you).
  • Social Security #: Number only (Card not required).
  • Name Change Info: Marriage Cert (if applicable).

Photo Compliance

  • Size: 2x2 Inches (US Standard).
  • Background: Plain White only.
  • Attire: No uniforms or camouflage.
  • No Glasses: Must be removed.
