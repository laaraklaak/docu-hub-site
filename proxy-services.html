Transparency First

When a Proxy Isn't Permitted.

Government regulations dictate that certain processes—like first-time passport applications or specific biometric captures—simply cannot be handled by a third party.

If your request requires an in-person appearance, we won't leave you to figure it out alone. We equip you with a Custom Guide and Pre-Submission Checklist. We outline exactly where to go, what formats to bring, and what fees to expect so you get approved on your first try.
