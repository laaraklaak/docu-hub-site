Requirements Checklist

What You Need Before We Start

  • Private Vehicle Only Must be registered as a private motor vehicle (white license plate). Commercial vehicles and public passenger vehicles require physical Tax Office visits.
  • Active Certificate of Fitness Your vehicle fitness must be currently valid and logged in the TAJ/Depot database.
  • Active Certificate of Insurance Your insurance policy must be active and updated in the central insurance repository.
  • CRITICAL: No Outstanding Traffic Tickets TAJ will strictly block renewals if you have unpaid traffic fines or missed court dates on your driver record. Lost your ticket? Request a free ticket lookup below ↓
