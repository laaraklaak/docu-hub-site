Motor Vehicle License Renewal

A secure, 100% digital process to clear your Jamaican road tax without queuing at the tax office. Please note: Docu-Hub exclusively processes renewals for Private Vehicles.

Before You Begin: The Pre-Audit Checklist

To ensure an overnight turnaround and prevent the TAJ portal from locking out your application, you must have the following documents ready before initiating contact with our proxy desk:

  • Up-to-Date Certificate of Fitness Your vehicle must possess an active, unexpired Certificate of Fitness recorded in the government database.
  • Active Insurance Cover Note You must provide proof of an active Jamaican motor vehicle insurance policy that matches the vehicle chassis and plate details.
  • Valid Owner Identification A clear copy of the registered owner's valid Government ID (Driver's License, Passport, or National ID).
  • Owner's TRN The official 9-digit Tax Registration Number (TRN) of the registered vehicle owner.

Critical Expiration Rule

Your Fitness and Insurance dates must not expire before your requested license period ends. For example, if you are applying for a 1-Year License, but your Certificate of Fitness expires in 8 months, the TAJ database will automatically reject the application. Ensure your supporting documents cover the full duration of the license you are purchasing.

Official TAJ Agency Fees (Private Vehicles)

The Jamaican government allows you to renew your private motor vehicle license for 6 months, 1 year, or the newly introduced 24-month (2-year) period. Fees are calculated strictly by your vehicle's engine size (CC rating).

The Digital Traffic Jam: Why Use a Proxy?

The TAJ online portal is completely free to use, and for a system built to create convenience, it can easily turn into an administrative nightmare for busy professionals. Without a deep understanding of the strict data requirements, clearing your Jamaican road tax online is often a frustrating gamble.

Consider Richard, a busy IT project manager. Wanting to avoid the physical lines at the tax office, he opted to use the free online portal. What should have taken five minutes turned into a two-day headache of alphanumeric lockouts. The TAJ requires exact inputs of your 17-character chassis number. Richard typed what he saw on his paper, confusing the letter 'O' with the number '0'. After three failed attempts, the system locked him out completely.

When he finally logged back in, he hit a "Hard Stop." The TAJ system is directly integrated with the Traffic Ticket Management System (TTMS). A family member who borrowed his car months ago had received a camera ticket. Because the ticket was tied to his vehicle's record, he was forced to navigate a completely different portal, clear the violation, and wait helplessly for the two government databases to sync before he could proceed.
