What If My Passport Gets Washed or Torn?

Docu-Hub Advisory • 3 Min Read

It happens to the best of us. A jacket gets tossed into the washing machine without checking the pockets. A young child finds a passport and accidentally tears a page. Pulling out a water-logged or defaced travel document is a sinking feeling.

While it is certainly nothing to panic over, a damaged passport is legally invalid for international travel. If you attempt to travel with a book that has a peeling data page, smeared ink, or a warped cover, you will be flagged by immigration authorities.

Why You Cannot Renew It Online

When you require a standard renewal, PICA allows you to process it efficiently through their online portal. However, if your book is damaged, the online portal is strictly off-limits.

Under Jamaican law, the physical integrity of a travel document is a matter of strict national security. PICA agents must physically inspect the book to ensure that the damage was purely accidental, and not an attempt to fraudulently tamper with or alter the document's identity data.

Do Not Throw the Book Away!

The single biggest mistake clients make is throwing away the damaged book out of frustration. You MUST keep and physically surrender the damaged passport to PICA when you apply for your replacement.

The Replacement Protocol

To secure a replacement for a damaged passport, you are required to complete three specific bureaucratic steps:

  • 01

    Surrender the Physical Book

    The damaged passport must be physically submitted along with your new application materials at PICA headquarters.

  • 02

    Provide a Sworn Affidavit

    You must sign an official legal statement—sworn before a Notary Public or Justice of the Peace—explaining the exact circumstances of how the damage occurred. This puts your accidental explanation on the legal record.

  • 03

    Submit Standard Materials

    Just like a new application, you will need to provide your original birth certificate, valid government ID, and two newly certified passport photos.
