Helpful Reference Info

Important details to keep in mind before submitting.

Which tax offices offer the fastest processing?

For the fastest turnaround (often completed same-day or within hours, depending on system uptime and crowd volume), TAJ operates specialized collection hubs and major centres, including:

  • Kingston: Constant Spring Revenue Service Centre & Cross Roads Tax Office
  • St. Andrew: Andrew’s Avenue Drop-Off Hub
  • St. Catherine: Spanish Town & Portmore Revenue Service Centres
  • Montego Bay: Montego Bay Revenue Service Centre

Note: These major hubs capture your digital signature and photo directly on-site. Processing times depend strictly on TAJ daily volume and system availability.

What happens if I use a smaller, local tax office?

If you choose to renew at a smaller or local tax office outside of the main hubs, please note:

  • Passport Photos Required: You MUST bring 2 physical passport-sized photographs (stamped and certified according to TAJ guidelines). Smaller offices do not always have on-site digital photo capture.
  • Extended Wait Time: The process will take longer (often several days to weeks) as documentation must be sent to a centralized printing facility before the physical licence is returned to your local office.
What standard documents do I need to prepare?
  • Your current (expired or expiring) Driver's Licence.
  • Your Tax Registration Number (TRN).
  • Completed TAJ Application Form F7.
  • Medical Certificate (required if over 60 years old or holding a commercial licence).
  • 2 Certified Passport Photos (if applying via a local non-hub office).
Can someone pick up my Driver's Licence for me?

Because the Tax Administration of Jamaica (TAJ) requires physical identity verification and signature capture, the cardholder must appear in person for final pickup. Our proxy service handles all the heavy lifting of form preparation and portal organization beforehand so your in-person pickup is as fast and smooth as possible.
