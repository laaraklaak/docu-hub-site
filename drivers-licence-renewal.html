How It Works

Three simple steps. No portals, no cashier lines.

1 Send Me Your Details

There is no form filling for you. Simply send me a clear photo of your licence (Front & Back) on WhatsApp, and I will verify your record and professionally prepare your official TAJ Form F7.

  • Current Licence: A clear photo of the Front & Back
  • TRN: Your Tax Registration Number
View additional details needed for Form F7

To ensure your Form F7 is 100% accurate and accepted by TAJ, please also text me:

  • Place of Birth: Parish or Country
  • Occupation: Current job title
  • Telephone Number: Active contact number
  • Email Address: Active email
  • Nationality: e.g., Jamaican
  • Current Address: (Only if changed from your old licence)

2 I Process Payment & Paperwork

Once I receive your details, I immediately log into the TAJ portal, process your renewal payment, and type up your physical F7 forms.

What you receive back from me:

A print-ready PDF containing your officially typed Form F7 and your verified TAJ Payment Receipt. This receipt immediately legally clears you to drive.

3 You Print, Drop-Off & Collect

Print the PDF package I send you and attach the receipt to your old licence. Simply walk into the Tax Office and drop it off at the Renewal Counter—bypassing the cashier entirely.

What if you use a smaller, local Tax Office?

You can drop your package at any TAJ branch across the island (like Linstead, Falmouth, or Morant Bay). Because these smaller offices don't print on-site, it usually takes 2 to 4 weeks for your card to arrive. However, your official 60-day receipt means you are 100% legally covered to drive while you wait!
