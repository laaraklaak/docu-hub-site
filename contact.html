How Our Service Works

We designed our process to be as simple and secure as possible. No confusing government jargon, and no waiting in lines.

1

Submit Your Request

Click "Open Secure Intake Portal" below to fill out our initial form. Tell us exactly what document or service you need assistance with.

2

Expert Desk Review

Our local team will immediately review your details to ensure you meet all government requirements before you are asked to pay any fees or courier physical documents.

3

Execution & Delivery

Once cleared, we act as your proxy to physically process your application at the agency in Jamaica and dispatch your final records via secure courier to your door.
