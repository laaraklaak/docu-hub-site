Registry FAQ

Why was my photo rejected?

PICA is strict about background shadows and hair covering ears. We pre-vet every photo before submission.

Data Security (JDPA)?

We purge digital ID copies 30 days after case closure. We adhere strictly to the 2020 Data Protection Act.

Can I pay cash?

To comply with POCA (Anti-Money Laundering), we require digital trails (Bank Transfer/Deposit) for all transactions.

Complex Case?

Estate records, multiple TRN issues, or corporate bulk needs often require a conversation.
