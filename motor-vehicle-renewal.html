Do It Yourself

Want to try the Tax Office website yourself?

If you want to try navigating the government website on your own, here is what they expect you to figure out.

What they expect you to have ready:

  • Up-to-date Fitness: Your fitness must already be active in the system.
  • Up-to-date Insurance: Your insurance company must have already updated your status in the system.
  • Private Registration: Valid only for private motor vehicles (white licence plates). Commercial or PPV vehicles still have to go stand in the physical line.

The Online Maze

  • Step 1: Go to the Tax Office website, find the "eServices" section, and set up an account.
  • Step 2: On your main dashboard, try to locate the "Motor Vehicle Services" section and click "Renew Motor Vehicle Licence".
  • Step 3: Carefully type in your chassis number, licence plate, and old certificate details. (If the spacing is off, the system will reject it).
  • Step 4: Enter your Visa or Mastercard details to pay the fee.
  • Step 5: Wait for the automated confirmation email, find the download link inside, and save your new digital registration document to your device.
