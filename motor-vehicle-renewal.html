02 Complete DIY TAJ Portal Guide (At Home)

If you want to handle your motor vehicle licence renewal independently online, follow this step-by-step breakdown to navigate the Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) eServices portal from your computer or smartphone.

1 What You Need First (Prerequisites)

  • Active Certificate of Fitness: Your vehicle must have a valid fitness logged in the islandwide depot database.
  • Active Certificate of Insurance: Your insurance must be active and updated in the central insurance database.
  • Private Registration: Valid only for private motor vehicles (white licence plates). Commercial/PPV vehicles require physical Tax Office processing.
  • Payment & Email: A valid Visa or Mastercard debit/credit card and an active personal email address.

2 Step-by-Step TAJ Portal Navigation

  • Step 1: Create / Log In to TAJ eServices: Visit the official TAJ portal (jamaicatax.gov.jm). Click "eServices" and create a user account, or log into your existing account.
  • Step 2: Locate Vehicle Renewal: On your main account dashboard, navigate to the "Motor Vehicle Services" section and click "Renew Motor Vehicle Licence".
  • Step 3: Input Vehicle Details: Enter the required information directly from your current/expired certificate:
    • Licence Plate Number
    • Chassis / VIN Number
    • Vehicle Registration / Certificate Number
    • Desired Expiry Period (6 Months or 12 Months)
  • Step 4: Enter Payment: Enter your card details to process the official TAJ government fee.
  • Step 5: Confirmation Email & Link: Upon successful payment, TAJ sends an official payment receipt to your email containing a direct redirect link.
  • Step 6: Download eMVRC: Click the redirect link inside the TAJ confirmation email to generate, view, and save your official electronic Motor Vehicle Registration Certificate (eMVRC) PDF.
CRITICAL: Unpaid Traffic Tickets Will Block Renewal! TAJ will strictly reject or block your online renewal if your driver record has unpaid traffic fines or missed court dates. Check your status before attempting portal filing.

03 Roadside Compliance (Staying Legal)

Tax Administration Jamaica no longer issues physical windshield sticker labels for private motor vehicle renewals. Understanding how to properly store and present your eMVRC e-Certificate keeps you fully compliant with the Jamaica Constabulary Force (JCF) traffic division.

1 Essential Driver Guidelines

  • Print a Glove Compartment Copy: Print a physical paper copy of your downloaded eMVRC PDF and store it in your vehicle's glove compartment at all times.
  • Keep a Smartphone Copy: Save a copy of the eMVRC PDF file directly on your mobile device for quick access.
  • Traffic Stops: During JCF police stops, presenting either the printed paper eMVRC or showing the PDF on your smartphone satisfies legal vehicle registration requirements.
