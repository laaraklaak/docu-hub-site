PREAMBLE

By submitting an intake form, application, or request through Docu-Hub Operations Services ("The Agency"), you ("The Client") agree to be bound by the following terms. These terms constitute a legally binding agreement regulating the relationship between the Client and the Agency regarding the processing of government documents and the curation of custom memorial tributes.

1. Scope of Services & Identity

1.1 No Government Affiliation: Docu-Hub is a private administrative logistics and design firm. We are not an extension of the Passport, Immigration & Citizenship Agency (PICA), Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ), or the Registrar General's Department (RGD).

1.2 Authorized Proxy: For government processes, engaging our services appoints Docu-Hub as your "Authorized Bearer" or "Proxy" to transact business with government bodies exclusively on your behalf.

2. Fees & Financial Policy

2.1 Service & Design Fees: Our "Service Fee" compensates the Agency for professional time, pre-submission audits, digital formatting, curation, and logistical execution. This fee is strictly non-refundable once our administrative or design processes have commenced.

2.2 Statutory/Government Fees: Statutory fees (e.g., taxes, passport fees) are collected by us as "Client Funds" and paid directly to the relevant agency. Should an application be rejected by a government entity, that entity may or may not refund this portion based on their own internal policies. Docu-Hub bears no liability for withheld government funds.

3. Client Obligations

3.1 Application Accuracy: The Client is solely responsible for the accuracy of all information provided via our intake forms. Docu-Hub is not liable for rejections, delays, or reprints caused by incorrect spelling, omitted dates, or hidden legal information provided by the Client.

3.2 Document Integrity: The Client warrants that all physical and digital documents provided (e.g., Old Passports, Birth Certificates) are genuine. Docu-Hub reserves the right to terminate services immediately without refund if forged or altered documents are suspected.

3.3 Memorial Content Rights: For Memorial Formatting services, the Client warrants that they have the right and permission to use all photographs, poems, and texts submitted for curation. Docu-Hub accepts no liability for copyright infringement claims arising from client-supplied materials.

4. Compliance & Data Privacy

Notice of Compliance: In adherence to the Proceeds of Crime Act (POCA) and Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA), we reserve the right to verify client identity prior to transaction execution. Sensitive records are permanently purged from our operational servers thirty (30) days following the completion of your service. For full details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

5. Limitation of Liability

Docu-Hub Operations Services shall not be held liable for delays, losses, or damages resulting from factors outside our direct control, including but not limited to:

  • Government Delays: System outages (e.g., TAJ or PICA network failures), backlogs, or processing delays by the issuing authority.
  • Officer Discretion: Rejection of an application by a Government Officer exercising their legal discretion.
  • Logistical Interruptions: Loss of travel tickets, missed appointments, or courier delays.
  • Print Variations (Memorials): Slight variations in color calibration between digital proofs viewed on mobile devices and final physical prints produced by third-party print shops.
  • Acts of God: Strikes, extreme weather events, or national emergencies.
