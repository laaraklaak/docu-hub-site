By submitting an application through Docu-Hub Operations Services ("The Agency"), you ("The Client") agree to be bound by the following terms. These terms constitute a legal agreement regulating the relationship between the Client and the Agency regarding the processing of government documents.

1. Private Agency Status

1.1 No Government Affiliation: Docu-Hub is a private logistics and administrative firm. We are not the Passport, Immigration & Citizenship Agency (PICA), Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ), or the Registrar General's Department (RGD).

1.2 Authorized Bearer: By engaging our services, you appoint Docu-Hub as your "Authorized Bearer" or "Agent" to transact business with government bodies on your behalf.

2. Fees & Financials

2.1 Service Fees: The "Service Fee" covers our professional time, consultation, data entry, and logistical execution. This fee is non-refundable once the vetting process has begun.

2.2 Government Fees: Statutory fees (taxes) are collected by us as "Client Funds" and paid directly to the government agency on your behalf. If an application is rejected by the government, the government may or may not refund this portion based on their own policies.

3. Compliance & Data Privacy

3.1 POCA (Anti-Money Laundering): In compliance with the Proceeds of Crime Act, we reserve the right to request valid government identification to verify the identity of all clients. We will not process transactions where the source of funds cannot be verified or appears illicit.

3.2 JDPA (Data Protection): In compliance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act (2020):

  • We collect your TRN and ID solely for the purpose of the requested transaction.
  • 30-Day Purge Rule: All copies of your sensitive documents (Birth Certs, ID Scans) are permanently deleted from our operational servers 30 days after your file is closed.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except the relevant Government Agencies required to complete your job.

4. Client Obligations

4.1 Accuracy: The Client is responsible for the accuracy of all information provided. Docu-Hub is not liable for rejections caused by incorrect spelling, wrong dates, or hidden information provided by the Client.

4.2 Document Integrity: The Client warrants that all physical documents provided (Old Passports, Birth Certificates) are genuine and authentic. Docu-Hub reserves the right to terminate services immediately if forged documents are suspected.

5. Limitation of Liability

Docu-Hub Operations Services shall not be held liable for delays, losses, or damages caused by:

  • Government system outages (e.g., PICA website crash or Tax Office network failure).
  • Strikes, natural disasters, or "Acts of God."

  • Loss of travel tickets or appointments due to government processing delays.
