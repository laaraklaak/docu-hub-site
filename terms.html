Preamble

By engaging with Docu-Hub Operations Services ("The Agency") for creative commissions or concierge logistics, you ("The Client") agree to be bound by the following terms. These terms constitute a legally binding agreement regulating the processing of government documents and the curation of bespoke prints.

1. Scope of Services & Identity

1.1 No Government Affiliation: Docu-Hub Operations Services is a private administrative logistics firm and design studio. We are not an extension of the Passport, Immigration & Citizenship Agency (PICA), Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ), or any other government department.

1.2 Authorized Proxy: For Concierge Logistics, engaging our services appoints Docu-Hub as your "Authorized Bearer" or "Proxy" to navigate bureaucracy and transact business with government bodies exclusively on your behalf.

1.3 Curation Studio: Under our Keepsakes division, Docu-Hub acts as an independent design studio curating custom physical prints and memorial layouts based on client instructions.

2. Fees & Financial Policy

2.1 Administrative Service Fees: For Concierge Logistics, our Service Fee compensates the Agency for professional time, pre-submission audits, and logistical execution (e.g., waiting in lines at TAJ). This fee is strictly non-refundable once our administrative processes have commenced.

2.2 Keepsakes & Custom Designs: Purchases of custom-printed invitations, memorial booklets, and tribute cards are final. Because these physical products are uniquely printed with client-specific data and photography, we cannot issue refunds once printing production begins.

2.3 Statutory/Government Fees: Statutory fees (e.g., eMVRC renewal costs) are collected as "Client Funds" and paid directly to the relevant government agency. Docu-Hub bears no liability for government funds withheld due to rejected applications.

3. Client Obligations & Warranties

3.1 Application Accuracy: The Client is solely responsible for the accuracy of all biographic and vehicle information provided via our intake procedures. Docu-Hub is not liable for government rejections caused by incorrect or omitted legal information.

3.2 Curation Content: For Keepsakes and Memorials, the Client warrants they have the legal right to use all provided photographs, family histories, and text. Docu-Hub is not liable for copyright infringement claims arising from client-supplied curatorial materials.

3.3 Proof Approval: For bespoke printing, the Client will be provided a digital proof. Once the proof is approved by the Client, Docu-Hub is not responsible for typos, layout errors, or omissions discovered in the final physical print.

4. Compliance & Data Privacy

Notice of Compliance: In adherence to the Proceeds of Crime Act (POCA) and Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA), sensitive administrative records handled during Concierge Logistics are permanently purged from our operational servers thirty (30) days following the successful delivery of your documents. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for complete details.

5. Limitation of Liability

Docu-Hub Operations Services shall not be held liable for delays, losses, or damages resulting from factors outside our direct control, including but not limited to:

  • Government Delays: System outages, backlogs, or slow processing times by issuing authorities (e.g., TAJ systems being offline).
  • Courier Delays: Delays caused by third-party delivery services when returning finalized documents or delivering physical keepsakes.
  • Print Variations: Slight variations in color calibration between digital proofs viewed on mobile devices and the final ink applied to physical stationery.
  • Acts of God: Hurricanes, strikes, extreme weather events, or national emergencies preventing the execution of logistics.
