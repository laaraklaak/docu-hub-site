The Process

How Proxy Lodgment Works

1

Pre-Audit & Prep

Select your service and complete our secure digital intake. We draft your complex government forms and audit your supporting documents for compliance.

2

Physical Lodgment

Once you courier your signed originals to us, our dedicated agents queue in person and hand-deliver your application directly to the agency desk.

3

Secure Global Delivery

We monitor the processing timeline, retrieve your freshly issued documents, and dispatch them to your overseas address via signature-required courier.
