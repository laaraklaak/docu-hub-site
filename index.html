Addressing Your Concerns

Frequently Asked Questions

I live overseas. Do I have to mail my physical original documents?

For most services (like TRN or standard RGD renewals), no. We only require certified high-resolution uploads via our secure client portal. If a specific agency demands a physical original (e.g., an expired passport for PICA), we will provide you with secure routing instructions to ensure its safety.

What happens if my application is rejected by the government agency?

This is exactly why our Digital Intake step exists. We audit all your uploads before submission. If an agency still flags a document or requires additional proof, we act as your local representative to rectify the issue at the office without requiring you to restart the process from overseas.

My situation is complex (e.g., lost passport or old TRN). Can you still help?

Yes. While our homepage highlights our most common services, we regularly handle complex and non-standard administrative matters. Simply select the most relevant service and reach out to us for a custom assessment of your case.
