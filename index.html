Online Eligibility Rules

  • You were an adult (18+) when your current passport was issued.
  • It is an "A" series book (post-Sept 2001).

Damaged passports strictly do not qualify for online renewal. They require physical inspection and an official Police Report.

Portal Help & FAQs

Direct answers to the most common Jamaican government portal errors.

Why is my US/UK bank card saying "Transaction Failed" on the PICA portal?

This is the #1 error for the diaspora. The PICA payment gateway uses a specific regional security protocol called 3D Secure. Overseas banks (like Chase, Bank of America, or Barclays) often flag this as a foreign fraud attempt and block it instantly to "protect" you, returning a generic error on the portal.

The Fix: If you are blocked at checkout, Docu-Hub can step in as your proxy and process the transaction securely via local Jamaican banking channels so your application draft isn't lost.

My PICA passport application is "locked" as a draft and won't let me pay. What do I do?

If your internet drops, or a payment fails, the PICA portal automatically "locks" your draft to prevent duplicate charges. The system typically holds this lock for 1 to 2 hours before releasing it.

The Fix: Do not start a brand new application, or it may flag you for duplicate submissions. Wait 2 hours, log back in, and try again. If it remains permanently locked, we can assist in clearing the cache and executing a gateway rescue.

Can I renew a damaged, washed, or torn passport online?

Absolutely not. PICA strictly prohibits online renewals for damaged passports because the agency must physically inspect and apply an official cancellation stamp to the ruined book.

The Fix: You must mail in the damaged passport, an original birth certificate, an official Police Report explaining the damage, and a notarized physical application. Contact us to coordinate this secure mail-in process.

Why is the portal asking for a Marriage Certificate if I just want to renew my passport?

If you are a married woman and your current passport is in your maiden name, PICA requires you to establish the legal name change. You cannot skip this step online.

The Fix: You must upload a high-resolution scan of your official Marriage Certificate alongside your application. If you were divorced, you must provide the Decree Absolute.

Why is the RGD portal asking for a "Birth Entry Number" I don't have?

The digital RGD portal requires the alpha-numeric Birth Entry Number (found on the top left of old certificates) to instantly query the database. If you leave this blank, the system forces an expensive and delayed "manual paper search."

The Fix: Docu-Hub can act as your proxy to conduct this search locally and securely pull your records without you paying the international portal premium.
