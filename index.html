Addressing Your Concerns

Frequently Asked Questions

I live overseas. Is it safe to send my original documents for proxy processing?

Absolutely. We prioritize document security above all else. For digital applications, we only require certified high-resolution uploads via our encrypted drive. When physical originals are required by agencies like PICA or RGD, we utilize trackable, signature-required global dispatch and store your files in locked, confidential environments. You receive real-time status updates from receipt to return.

What happens if there is an error on my application or an agency rejects my files?

That is precisely why clients choose Docu-Hub. Before any document reaches PICA, TAJ, RGD, or NIS, our team conducts a complete pre-submission audit to verify names, dates, notarizations, and photo compliance. If an agency-flagged issue arises, we manage the correction process directly with the institution on your behalf to ensure your file proceeds smoothly.

My situation is complex (e.g., lost passport or old TRN lookup). Can you still help?

Yes. While our homepage highlights our most requested online renewals and record searches, we regularly handle complex and non-standard administrative matters. If your situation involves police reports, replacement applications, or custom agency authorization, simply submit an inquiry through our secure portal, and we will assess your case during a private consultation.
