About The Founder

Why I Started Docu-Hub.

For 15 years, I worked inside the banking system at NCB. In that world, if one digit was wrong on a form, the whole transaction stopped. I learned that "details matter."

But I also saw regular people—hardworking Jamaicans—losing entire days of pay just to stand in a line at the Tax Office or PICA, only to be turned away because they forgot one photocopy or filled out the form in the wrong color ink.

It didn't seem fair.

I created Docu-Hub to be the bridge. When you hand your documents to me, I treat them with the same security and care as I treated bank files. I check them. I fix the errors. I stand in the line.

We are a registered business, compliant with the new Data Protection Act (JDPA). That means your ID doesn't sit on a phone; it gets processed and then securely deleted.

You have better things to do than wait in line. Let me handle the red tape.
