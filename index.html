Portal Help & FAQs

Quick fixes and insider knowledge for the most common PICA and TAJ government portal errors.

The TAJ portal keeps blocking my vehicle renewal, but won't say why.

If the portal blocks your payment without a specific error code, 90% of the time it is caused by an outstanding traffic ticket. The TAJ system will not show you your tickets directly.

The Fix: You must visit the separate Traffic Ticket Lookup portal to check your license status and clear your name before TAJ will accept your payment.

I have my paper Certificate of Fitness, but TAJ says it's invalid.

The TAJ portal relies on a live digital sync. Even if you have the physical paper, your insurance broker or the Island Traffic Authority (ITA) must digitally upload your active dates to the central database.

The Fix: Contact your insurance broker to ensure they have pushed your active policy dates to the national database. If the digital dates don't match exactly, the portal remains locked.

Why is my overseas bank card saying "Transaction Failed"?

Overseas banks frequently flag the PICA and TAJ portals' 3D-Secure gateways as fraud and block them instantly to protect you. If you try more than 3 times, the portals may lock your draft entirely.

The Fix: Begin registration with us. We will step in as your proxy and process the payment securely via local Jamaican channels.

Can I renew a damaged, washed, or torn passport online?

No. PICA strictly prohibits online renewals for damaged books because the agency must physically inspect and apply a cancellation stamp.

The Fix: You must submit a physical mail-in application. We coordinate this strict manual protocol safely from your location.
