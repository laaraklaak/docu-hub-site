About The Founder

Why I Started Docu-Hub.

Having spent over 15 years working within Jamaica’s corporate banking sector, I learned one rule above all others: details matter. In that environment, a single missing digit or unchecked box could stop a transaction cold.

But outside the bank, I saw a different reality. I saw hardworking professionals losing entire days of pay just to stand in lines, or struggling to navigate confusing online government portals.

I knew there had to be a more efficient way.

I created Docu-Hub to bridge the gap between you and the red tape. When you entrust your documents to me, I apply the same banking-grade security and precision to your files that I practiced for over a decade.

We are a registered business and fully compliant with the Data Protection Act (JDPA). This means your sensitive information isn't just "on a phone"—it is processed securely and protected at all times.
