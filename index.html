Frequently Asked Questions

I live overseas. Do I have to mail my physical original documents?

It depends on the service. For standard RGD searches, high-resolution digital uploads are sufficient. However, for complex processes like an NIS Claim or surrendering a damaged passport, the Jamaican government strictly requires wet-signed original documents. We will guide you exactly on what needs to be securely couriered via DHL.

What happens if my application is rejected by the government agency?

This is exactly why our Pre-Audit step exists. We meticulously audit your uploads and forms before submission to prevent rejections. If an agency still flags a document or requires additional proof, we act as your local representative to rectify the issue at the office without requiring you to restart the process from overseas.

How do I know my documents are secure with you?

Docu-Hub Operations is legally registered with the Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ). We utilize secure intake portals, and any final printed records (like a Birth Certificate or Passport) are never sent via standard mail—they are exclusively dispatched via signature-required international couriers.
