Who we are & how it works.

I am Laura Clarke, founder of Docu-Hub Operations. We are an independent, private administrative proxy service—we are not affiliated with any Jamaican government agency.

Our process is simple: we review your requirements, prepare the exact paperwork needed, and handle the submission process where a proxy is available. We remove the guesswork so you can get it done faster.

Secure Onboarding

For your security, all onboarding is handled directly through a scheduled consultation or our verified WhatsApp. We never collect payments or sensitive records through this website.
