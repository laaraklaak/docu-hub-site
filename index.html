Passport Assistance

Streamlined guidance for Jamaican passport applications and renewals for overseas residents.

Watch: Portal Walkthrough

The complete 3-gateway blueprint to completing your renewal seamlessly.

Online Eligibility Rules

1. Who Qualifies to Apply Online:
• You were an adult (18+) when your current passport was issued.
• It is an "A" series book (post-Sept 2001).
• It is physically intact (not lost/damaged).
• Your name has not changed.

2. Pre-Application Checklist:
Have your current passport, active email address, digital JPEG photo, and a valid MasterCard/Visa ready.

Gateway Warning: Overseas bank cards are frequently blocked by foreign fraud filters. If declined, we can execute local channel processing.

Payment Rescue
Damaged Passports (Mail-In)

Damaged passports strictly do not qualify for online renewal. PICA requires physical inspection and an official cancellation stamp.

• Required: Original damaged passport, original birth certificate, an official Police Report, and a notarized application package.

Vital Records

Secure sourcing of official Jamaican Birth, Marriage, and Death Certificates via the RGD.

Birth Certificate Fields

To request a certified copy via the new online portal, you must have:

• Full Legal Name at birth.
• Birth Entry Number: Critical to avoid manual search fees.
• Temporal Data: Sex and exact Date of Birth.
• Spatial Data: Name of hospital/address, Parish, and District.
• Parental ID: Full names, including mother's exact maiden name.

Search Proxy
Marriage Cert Fields

Physical verification scans are required to be uploaded instantly alongside the web form fields:

• Core Data: Full legal names, Date, and Place of Marriage.
• Required Uploads: Scans of approved Minister's License and valid government IDs.
• Dissolution Proof: Decree Absolutes if previously married.

Vehicle Licensing

Complete virtual management for 100% paperless tax office renewals and gateway rescue.

Watch: eMVRC Walkthrough

How to map out the TAJ payment process and generate your certificate.

Gateway Blueprint

Step 1: The Roadblock Check
Clear any outstanding e-tickets or unpaid court fines via the traffic portal first.

Step 2: Gateway 1 (TAJ)
Process payment using Owner's TRN, Plate Number, and Chassis Number. Instantly save the TAJ Receipt Number.

Step 3: Gateway 2 (CGDS)
Key in the Chassis Number and the TAJ Receipt Number into the secondary portal. Hit submit to generate and email the digital PDF of your Electronic Motor Vehicle Registration Certificate (eMVRC).

Stuck at the TAJ Gateway? If a credit card triggers fraud filters, we handle the full sequence utilizing local payment infrastructure.
