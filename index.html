Portal Help & FAQs

Direct answers to the most common Jamaican government portal errors.

Why is my US/UK bank card saying "Transaction Failed" on the PICA portal?

This is the #1 error for the diaspora. The PICA payment gateway uses a specific regional security protocol called 3D Secure. Overseas banks flag this as a foreign fraud attempt and block it instantly to protect you.

The Fix: Book an appointment with us. Docu-Hub can step in as your proxy and process the transaction securely via local Jamaican banking channels.

My PICA passport application is "locked" as a draft and won't let me pay. What do I do?

If your internet drops, or a payment fails, the PICA portal automatically "locks" your draft to prevent duplicate charges. The system typically holds this lock for 1 to 2 hours before releasing it.

The Fix: Wait 2 hours, log back in, and try again. If it remains permanently locked, schedule an intake call and we can assist in clearing the cache and executing a gateway rescue.

Can I renew a damaged, washed, or torn passport online?

Absolutely not. PICA strictly prohibits online renewals for damaged passports because the agency must physically inspect and apply an official cancellation stamp to the ruined book.

The Fix: You must submit a manual mail-in application. Book a spot on our calendar to safely coordinate this secure physical processing from your location.

Why is the RGD portal asking for a "Birth Entry Number" I don't have?

The digital RGD portal requires the alpha-numeric Birth Entry Number to instantly query the database. If you leave this blank, the system forces an expensive and delayed manual paper search.

The Fix: Docu-Hub can act as your proxy to conduct this search locally and securely pull your records without you paying international portal premiums.

How long does it take for a mailed-in passport to return from the Embassy in Washington or London?

The traditional Embassy route is slow. Applications mailed to the Embassy in Washington D.C. or London are grouped and shipped back to PICA in Kingston. Standard processing time is 10 to 12 weeks.

The Docu-Hub Advantage: Unless your passport is severely damaged and requires physical surrender, applying online with our compliance review cuts this wait time down to roughly 20 working days.

Can I expedite my Jamaican passport renewal if I apply online?

While PICA does not offer rush fees for overseas portal applicants, applying online is inherently the expedited route.

An online renewal bypasses the Embassy entirely. PICA processes compliant overseas digital applications in approximately 20 working days, printing and dispatching it directly to your international address via DHL.

Do I have to mail my original Jamaican birth certificate for a manual renewal?

Yes. If you are required to submit a manual, paper-based application, PICA strictly requires your original birth certificate and/or marriage certificate. Notarized copies will trigger an automatic rejection.

If a manual application is mandatory, Docu-Hub coordinates secure, tracked courier logistics to ensure your irreplaceable original documents are handled safely and returned intact.
