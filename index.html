Liaison Profile

Laura Clarke

Principal Liaison & Proprietor

Compliance is my currency. My career began in the rigorous environment of Jamaica's banking sector (National Commercial Bank), where precision and confidentiality aren't just options—they are mandates. I spent over 15 years mastering the art of financial administration, data privacy, and logistical coordination.

I founded Docu-Hub because I saw a gap in the market: Professionals and citizens were losing hours of their lives standing in lines at Tax Offices and PICA, often for simple transactions. I realized that what people needed wasn't just a "bearer"—they needed a Proxy. Someone who understands the paperwork better than they do.

Today, Docu-Hub operates as a premium bridge between you and the State. We don't just "drop off" forms; we audit them. We don't just "wait in line"; we strategize the fastest route to completion. When you hand me your documents, you are handing them to a professional who treats your identity with the same fiduciary care as a bank vault.
