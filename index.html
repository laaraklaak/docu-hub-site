Knowledge Base

Why staying legal saves you money

Why does a current Address matter?
If your Tax Office address is outdated, you will miss Traffic Ticket notifications. If a ticket goes unpaid for 30 days, it attracts penalties. We update your record during renewal.
Documents needed for Fitness?
To pass fitness, you need your original Registration Certificate (Title is not needed), valid Insurance Cover Note, and the vehicle must be present.
Can you clear Traffic Tickets?
Yes. We can query the database to find outstanding tickets. If they are standard tickets, we can pay them on your behalf.
