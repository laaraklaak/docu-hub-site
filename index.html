Online Eligibility Rules

• You were an adult (18+) when your current passport was issued.
• It is an "A" series book (post-Sept 2001).
• It is not lost or damaged.
• Your name has not changed.

Prerequisite checklists for the live application portal.

Birth Certificate Fields

• Birth Entry Number: Critical to avoid manual search fees.
• Spatial Data: Exact Hospital, Parish, and District.
• Parental ID: Mother's exact maiden name.
