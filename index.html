Portal Help & FAQs

Quick fixes and insider knowledge for the most common PICA and TAJ government portal errors.

The TAJ portal keeps blocking my vehicle renewal, but won't say why.

If the portal blocks your payment without a specific error code, 90% of the time it is caused by an outstanding traffic ticket.

The Fix: You must visit the separate Traffic Ticket Lookup portal to check your license status and clear your name.

I have my paper Certificate of Fitness, but TAJ says it's invalid.

The TAJ portal relies on a live digital sync. Even if you have the physical paper, your insurance broker or the ITA must digitally upload your active dates.

The Fix: Contact your insurance broker to ensure they have pushed your active policy dates to the national database.

Why is my overseas bank card saying "Transaction Failed"?

Overseas banks frequently flag the PICA and TAJ portals' gateways as fraud and block them instantly. If you try more than 3 times, the portals may lock your draft.

The Fix: Begin registration with us. We will step in as your proxy and process the payment securely via local channels.

Can I renew a damaged, washed, or torn passport online?

No. PICA strictly prohibits online renewals for damaged books because the agency must physically inspect and apply a cancellation stamp.

The Fix: You must submit a physical mail-in application. We coordinate this strict manual protocol safely.

Do you handle complex name amendments or record corrections?

Complex corrections usually require personal appearances and sworn declarations, so our team cannot act as a proxy for these specific cases.

The Solution: We offer a custom DIY Action Plan ($35 USD). We audit your case and provide the exact forms, steps, and scripts you need to successfully submit the correction yourself. Message us to book a consultation!
