National Insurance Scheme (NIS)

Before booking a consultation for NIS audits, voluntary sign-ups, or pension claims, please review the strict requirements and timelines below to manage your expectations.

The Reality of NIS Timelines

NIS processing is not an overnight service. It involves a highly manual government audit of historical records. Depending on your work history, a new retirement claim can take anywhere from 3 to 6+ months to fully process.

The "Must-Have" Identifiers

No application or audit can proceed without your NIS Number and your TRN (Tax Registration Number). If you do not have these numbers written down, this must be disclosed immediately so a search can be conducted.

Choose Your NIS Scenario

Locate your specific objective below to see exactly what documentation and history you need to compile before we begin processing your file.

1. Contribution Statement (Audit)

Requesting a formal update to see your recorded weeks.

  • NIS Number and TRN.
  • Valid Government ID.
  • Detailed Employment History: You must provide the exact names of all Jamaican employers you worked for, the approximate years worked, and any maiden/former names used during those periods.

2. Voluntary Contribution Sign-Up

Closing gaps in your record while living overseas or unemployed.

  • Must be under the official pensionable age.
  • Cannot be currently employed by a company inside Jamaica.
  • NIS Number and TRN.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate.
  • Completed Voluntary Contributor Application Form.

3. Retirement Pension Claim

Filing for your lifelong pension or one-time retirement grant.

  • The 520-Week Rule: A full lifelong pension requires a minimum of 520 weeks (10 years) of contributions.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate (to legally prove you have reached retirement age).
  • NIS Number and TRN.
  • Overseas Banking Form: The specific NIS direct deposit mandate must be completed by your international bank for wire transfers.

4. Funeral Grants & Survivor Benefits

Claiming death benefits for a deceased contributor.

  • Original RGD Death Certificate.
  • The deceased's Birth Certificate and NIS Number.
  • The Strict Receipt Rule: You must provide the original, itemized receipt from the funeral home. It must clearly state the applicant's name as the person who paid the bill.
