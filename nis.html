Contribution History Statement

Before applying for any benefits or making voluntary payments, you must know your current standing. We act as your proxy to formally request and retrieve your official, up-to-date Contribution History Statement from the MLSS.

Required Documents

  • Your official NIS Number & TRN
  • Copy of valid Government Photo ID
  • Signed Docu-Hub Proxy Authorization Form
  • Names/Dates of past Jamaican employers

The Workflow

  • Submit your identifiers via our secure digital intake.
  • Sign the Proxy Delegation Form sent to your email.
  • Our agents physically queue and request your ledger at the NIS office in Jamaica.
  • A certified digital scan of your statement is securely transmitted to you.

MLSS Gov Fee $0 USD
DHL Courier (If needed) $45 - $65 USD
* Statements are typically delivered digitally. DHL required only for physical originals. Request Statement
Voluntary Contributor Application

If you have migrated but want to reach the 520-week threshold to qualify for a full pension, you can apply to make ongoing voluntary contributions from overseas. We handle the strict setup process.

Required Documents

  • Recent NIS Contribution Statement
  • NIS Number, TRN, & Valid Passport
  • Completed Voluntary Contributor Application Form (Original Wet Signature)

The Workflow

  • We audit your current statement to ensure you are eligible.
  • We type and prepare your MLSS application forms.
  • You print, sign, and courier the original forms to our office in Jamaica.
  • We lodge the application and establish your approved payment schedule.
Docu-Hub Setup Fee $185 USD
MLSS Setup Fee $0 USD
DHL Courier (Estimated) $45 - $65 USD
* You are responsible for making your own ongoing contribution payments directly to the MLSS. Start Application
Retirement Pension Claim

For individuals reaching the age of 65. Whether you qualify for the ongoing monthly pension or the one-time Retirement Grant, claim forms must be flawlessly prepared, witnessed by a Notary, and physically submitted.

Required Documents

  • Original Birth Certificate
  • Original NIS Card & Valid Passport
  • Jamaican Bank Account Details (or approved remittance method)
  • Notarized MLSS Claim Forms

The Workflow (Wet Signature)

  • We digitally prepare your exact MLSS pension claim forms.
  • You print and sign the forms in the presence of a Notary Public overseas.
  • You courier the physical originals to our secure facility via DHL.
  • Our agents hand-deliver the claim to the NIS processing desk.
Docu-Hub Proxy Fee $215 USD
MLSS Claim Fee $0 USD
DHL Courier (Estimated) $45 - $65 USD
* The MLSS does not charge a fee to process a pension claim. Overseas Notary fees are your responsibility. Start Pension Claim
Funeral Grant Claim

If an active NIS contributor passes away, the family can claim a death benefit/funeral grant to assist with expenses. Strict original documentation proving relationship and expenses is required.

Required Documents

  • Official Death Certificate
  • Original Funeral Home Receipt (must be in applicant's name)
  • Proof of Relationship (Marriage/Birth Cert)
  • Deceased's NIS Card & Applicant's ID

The Workflow (Wet Signature)

  • We review your supporting documents for compliance.
  • We prepare the grant application form.
  • You sign the documents with a Notary and courier the physical packet to Jamaica.
  • We lodge the documents with the NIS and track the payout status.
Docu-Hub Proxy Fee $185 USD
MLSS Gov Fee $0 USD
DHL Courier (Estimated) $45 - $65 USD
* The MLSS does not charge a fee to process a grant claim. Courier rates are approximate. Start Grant Claim

Important: The Wet Signature Workflow

The Ministry of Labour and Social Security requires official, witnessed original documents to process claims. We do not simply email you a blank form to figure out on your own. To ensure your claim is not rejected by the agency, we follow a strict preparation protocol:

1

Digital Preparation: We collect your details online and professionally type your complex NIS forms to ensure exact MLSS compliance.

2

Print & Witness: We email you the completed PDF. You must print it and sign it (wet signature) in the presence of a Notary Public.

3

Physical Dispatch: You must place the signed original forms in a courier envelope (e.g., DHL) and mail them to our Jamaica office.

4

In-Person Proxy: We receive your physical documents and submit them by hand directly to the NIS office on your behalf.

Service Advisory

Why proxy lodgment is critical for the diaspora

Thousands of Jamaicans currently living in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada spent the first 10 to 15 years of their careers working in Jamaica. During that time, National Insurance Scheme (NIS) contributions were deducted from their paychecks.

Decades later, when it comes time to retire or track down a record, many people are frustrated to find that NIS processes are heavily reliant on physical paperwork, wet signatures, and manual ledger searches.

"Calling the NIS offices from overseas to track down decades-old employment records is incredibly difficult. Most older records require a manual, in-person request."

The Solution: You do not need to fly back to Jamaica to trace your history or file a claim. Docu-Hub Operations serves as your secure, on-the-ground administrative desk. We conduct the local inquiries, verify your eligibility, and meticulously prepare your claim forms so that when they are submitted by hand, they are processed correctly.
