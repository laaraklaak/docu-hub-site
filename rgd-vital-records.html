Helpful Reference Info

Required details and rules for each certificate type.

Birth Certificate Requirements

To locate and request an official Jamaican Birth Certificate, you must gather the following:

  • Full Name at Birth: First, middle, and last name recorded at birth.
  • Date & Place of Birth: Day, month, year, and parish of birth.
  • Mother's Full Maiden Name: Essential for database record matching.
  • Father's Full Name: Required if listed on the original entry.
  • Birth Entry Number (Optional but Recommended): Found on older paper copies; dramatically accelerates online lookup.
Marriage Certificate Requirements

To request a Marriage Certificate copy for passport updates, spousal visas, or legal matters:

  • Groom's Full Name: Prior to marriage.
  • Bride's Full Maiden Name: Prior to marriage.
  • Date & Venue of Marriage: Exact date, church/resort/venue, and parish.
  • Officiant's Name: Name of the Marriage Officer, Minister, or JP who officiated the ceremony.
Death Certificate Requirements

To secure a Death Certificate for settling estates or banking matters:

  • Full Legal Name of Deceased: First, middle, and last name.
  • Date & Place of Death: Day, month, year, hospital or residence address, and parish.
  • Age at Death & Gender: Age of deceased at passing.
  • Death Entry Number (Optional): Found on burial orders or older registry receipts.
Processing Speeds & Delivery Options

RGD certificate applications offer two main processing speeds:

  • Express Service: Approximately 7 to 10 working days.
  • Ordinary Service: Approximately 6 weeks.

Certificates can be scheduled for local RGD branch pickup or couriered directly to addresses within Jamaica or overseas.

What if someone must collect at an RGD branch on your behalf?

If you choose local branch pickup and use a proxy bearer, your designated proxy must present an unexpired Government Photo ID alongside a signed Authorization Letter from you stamped by a Justice of the Peace (JP).
