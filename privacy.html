Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Document ID: DH-POL-2026-04  |  Last Verified: April 2026

At Docu-Hub Operations Services, we operate a dual-framework studio balancing the creation of highly personal heirloom keepsakes with the strict, secure logistics required for government concierge services. This policy outlines our rigorous compliance framework for the collection, processing, and protection of client data under the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA).

Regulatory Framework: In facilitating government document compliance and concierge logistics, Docu-Hub Operations Services maintains strict administrative protocols to protect sensitive biographical and identification data.

1. Institutional Role & Identity

Docu-Hub Operations Services operates two distinct divisions to serve our clients:

  • Concierge Logistics: An independent administrative consultancy facilitating complex document navigation, including eMVRC vehicle renewals and general compliance logistics.
  • Bespoke Keepsakes: A design studio crafting premium, custom physical prints, including milestone invitations, memorial booklets, and tribute collections.
NOTICE: Docu-Hub Operations Services is an independent private entity based in St. Catherine. We are NOT a government agency or an arm of Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ), PICA, or the Registrar General’s Department (RGD). We act exclusively as your authorized liaison and private design curator.

2. Data Classification & Scope

To execute our bespoke services, we process the following categories of data:

  • Logistical Data (Concierge): Identity source documents (IDs, TRN), biographic metadata, vehicle registration details, and legal declarations required for government form fulfillment and eMVRC renewals.
  • Curatorial Data (Keepsakes): Personal multimedia (photos, obituaries, family histories, event details) provided by clients for the explicit purpose of designing bespoke invitations, memorial booklets, and printed tributes.
  • Administrative Data: Standard contact information (phone numbers, WhatsApp logs, addresses) required for client communication and the secure physical delivery of your finished portfolios.

3. Processing Rationale

Data processing is conducted strictly under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill our obligation to design your commissioned keepsakes or successfully navigate your requested government logistics.
  • Explicit Consent: Specifically regarding the processing of highly sensitive family memories, photographs, and tributes, which are utilized entirely upon your explicit request for design purposes.

4. Technical Security Infrastructure

Because we handle both precious family memories and sensitive government documentation, we deploy a strict separation strategy:

  • Secure Intake: All logistical documentation is received via secure, encrypted channels (such as authenticated WhatsApp business accounts or secure email) and is never intermingled with our public design portfolios.
  • Physical Security: All physical documents handled on your behalf are stored securely at our Linstead studio and returned to you via airtight, confidential delivery protocols.

5. Data Life-Cycle & Purge Policy

In accordance with the JDPA principle of storage limitation:

  • Logistical Purge: Digital copies of identification assets (TRN, IDs) used for government applications are permanently purged from our primary systems within 30 days of the successful completion and delivery of your concierge service.
  • Keepsake Archiving: Finalized design files (memorial booklets, invitations) are securely archived in our studio network should you require reprints in the future. You may request the permanent deletion of these design assets at any time.

6. Data Subject Rights

Under the JDPA, you maintain the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access to any personal data currently held within our systems.
  • Right to request immediate erasure of your digital design assets or logistical documentation.
  • Right to restrict processing or update your contact and delivery information.
