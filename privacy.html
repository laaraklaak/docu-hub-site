Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Docu-Hub Operations Services, we value the trust you place in us when handling your most sensitive personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data in our capacity as a professional administrative consultancy and document liaison.

1. Our Role and Identity

Docu-Hub Operations Services is a private administrative consultancy.

Disclaimer: We are NOT a government agency, nor are we affiliated with the Passport, Immigration and Citizenship Agency (PICA), the Registrar General’s Department (RGD), or any other government ministry. We act as an independent service provider facilitating applications on behalf of our clients.

2. Information We Collect

To provide document facilitation services, we must collect sensitive personal information, including:

  • Biographic Data: Full name, date of birth, profession, and marital status.
  • Contact Information: Physical and mailing addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses.
  • Identification Documents: Digital copies of Government IDs, Birth Certificates, Marriage Certificates, and current/expired Passports.
  • Supporting Documentation: Police reports, affidavits, and legal decrees (e.g., Deed Polls).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Completing official government application forms.
  • Verifying your identity to ensure application accuracy.
  • Communicating with you regarding the status of your application.
  • Coordinating delivery or pickup of completed documents.

4. Data Storage and Security

We utilize industry-leading security measures to protect your sensitive documents:

  • Secure Collection: We use Jotform as our primary document collection portal. All data submitted is protected by SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption during transit.
  • Cloud Storage: Collected documents are stored in secure, password-protected environments (such as Google Drive or Dropbox) with restricted access limited only to authorized Docu-Hub personnel.
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted at rest and in transit to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your data with:

  • Government Authorities: Only the specific information required by law to process your application (e.g., PICA, RGD, Tax Office).
  • Secure Processors: Reliable technology partners (Jotform, Google, Dropbox) that comply with global data protection standards.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your sensitive identification documents indefinitely:

  • Documents are retained only for the duration of the service contract.
  • Once your application is finalized and your documents are returned or collected, digital copies are purged from our primary storage systems within a reasonable timeframe (typically 30-60 days) unless otherwise required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the immediate deletion of your digital documents once a service is completed.
  • Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time (noting that this may result in the cancellation of pending services).
