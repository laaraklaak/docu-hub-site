Data Protection Framework

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Document ID: DH-POL-2026-02  |  Last Verified: March 2026

At Docu-Hub Operations Services, we operate a hybrid framework consisting of a high-security administrative gateway and a modern digital technology studio. This policy outlines our rigorous compliance framework for the collection, processing, and protection of client data under the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA).

Regulatory Framework: As a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP) handling civil registries, alongside our operations as a Digital Technology provider, Docu-Hub maintains strict Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT), and Data Privacy protocols.

1. Institutional Role & Identity

Docu-Hub operates dual divisions to serve our clients:

  • Operations Services: An independent administrative consultancy facilitating complex document logistics for local and international civil registries.
  • Docu-Hub Studio: A technology provider engineering Near Field Communication (NFC) Smart Hubs, digital business cards, Smart Medical Tags, and Memorial Keepsakes.
NOTICE: Docu-Hub is a private entity. We are NOT a government agency or an arm of the Passport, Immigration and Citizenship Agency (PICA) or the Registrar General’s Department (RGD). We act exclusively as an authorized liaison and private technology provider.

2. Data Classification & Scope

To execute our dual mandate, we process the following categories of data:

  • Administrative Data: Identity source documents (IDs, birth certificates), biographic metadata, and legal declarations required for government form fulfillment.
  • Digital Hub Assets: Public-facing information voluntarily provided for Smart Business Cards and Smart Menus, including professional links, portfolios, and contact architecture.
  • Memorial Keepsake Data: User-generated multimedia (photos, videos, obituaries) provided by families for hosting on digital tribute platforms.
  • Voluntary Medical Data: Basic health directives and emergency contact information voluntarily provided for encoding onto Smart Medical Tags.

3. NFC Architecture & Tap Analytics

Docu-Hub Studio utilizes passive Near Field Communication (NFC) microchips in our hardware. It is critical to understand how this technology interacts with your data:

  • Passive Hardware: Our NFC tags do not contain batteries or GPS modules. They cannot track your physical location or actively monitor your device.
  • Public Intent vs. Private Storage: When an NFC product (like a digital business card or memorial tag) is tapped, it transmits a secure URL that displays the digital profile you have authorized us to host. The hardware itself does not store your private intake documents.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Our digital hubs may collect basic, non-identifiable analytics (such as the number of times a card was tapped or the device operating system) to ensure platform functionality. We do not collect the personal data of the individuals tapping the tags.

4. Processing Rationale

Data processing is conducted strictly under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Completion of mandated government applications and the hosting of requested digital profiles.
  • Explicit Consent: Particularly regarding the processing of multimedia for memorials and emergency directives for medical tags, which are processed entirely upon the explicit request of the client.

5. Technical Security Infrastructure

We deploy a multi-layered defense-in-depth strategy to secure client information:

  • Secure Intake Pipeline: All sensitive intake data is ingested via 256-bit SSL encrypted channels.
  • Cloud Hosting Separation: Public-facing digital hub data (business cards, menus) is hosted on secure, high-availability servers separate from our internal encrypted administrative silos where sensitive government application data is stored.

6. Data Life-Cycle & Purge Policy

In accordance with the JDPA principle of storage limitation:

  • Administrative Purge: Digital identification assets used for government applications are flagged for permanent deletion and purged from our primary systems within 30 to 60 days of service finalization.
  • Digital Hub Hosting: Data hosted for Smart Business Cards, Medical Tags, and Memorials is retained for as long as your hosting subscription is active. Upon cancellation or formal request, all public-facing digital profiles are permanently taken offline and the data is erased.

7. Data Subject Rights

Under the JDPA, you maintain the following rights over your data:

  • Right of access to any personal data currently held within our systems or hosted on your digital hubs.
  • Right to request immediate erasure of digital identity captures or public-facing digital profiles.
  • Right to restrict processing or update the information encoded on your NFC hardware.
