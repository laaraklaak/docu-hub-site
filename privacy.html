Privacy & Cookie Policy

Transparency and security are the foundations of our agency. Learn how we manage, protect, and utilize your data.

Last Updated: August 2026 | Applies to Global Applicants

1. Introduction & Scope

At Docu-Hub Operations Services ("we," "us," or "our"), we understand that you are entrusting us with your most sensitive personal and legal documents. This Privacy Policy governs how we collect, use, protect, and delete your data when you use our website or engage our proxy services in Jamaica.

Our operations comply with the Jamaican Data Protection Act (DPA), ensuring that your Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is handled with the highest standard of legal security.

2. Information We Collect

To successfully navigate Jamaican government agencies (PICA, RGD, MLSS) on your behalf, we must collect specific information. This includes:

  • Identity Data: Full legal name, date of birth, place of birth, and Mother's Maiden Name.
  • Government Identifiers: Tax Registration Number (TRN), Passport Numbers, and National Insurance Scheme (NIS) Numbers.
  • Documentary Evidence: Scanned digital copies or physical originals of Birth Certificates, Marriage Certificates, Deed Polls, and Affidavits.
  • Contact Information: Your current overseas residential address, email address, and phone number for dispatch and communication.

3. How We Use Your Information

We operate on a strict policy of data minimalism. Your information is never sold, traded, or rented to third parties. Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To pre-fill and submit official government applications to PICA, RGD, or the MLSS.
  • To conduct authorized manual ledger searches at Jamaican administrative offices.
  • To communicate with you regarding application status or required clarifications.
  • To facilitate the secure DHL courier dispatch of your final printed records.

"Your digital footprint and uploaded documents are used solely to facilitate your specific service request. We do not retain unneeded data once your proxy job is successfully completed."

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust physical and digital security measures to protect your documents against unauthorized access. Digital uploads provided during our Intake Process are stored on encrypted, secure servers. Any physical documents mailed to our St. Catherine office are held in secure, locked filing systems accessible only by authorized proxy agents.

Once your final documents are couriered back to you and the job is marked as "Completed," we routinely purge your digital scans and temporary intake data from our active systems to prevent long-term exposure.

5. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They act as a short-term memory for the site, allowing us to ensure the platform functions efficiently. We do not use cookies to spy on you or access files on your device.

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These are essential for the website to function. They allow you to navigate the site, interact with our service hubs, and securely transition to our digital intake forms.
  • Performance & Analytics: We may use anonymous tools to count visits and traffic sources. This helps us understand which services are most in demand across the diaspora, allowing us to allocate our proxy resources effectively. This data is entirely aggregated and anonymous.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect the functionality of our website.

6. Your Rights & Contact

Under data protection guidelines, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccuracies, or request the premature deletion of your intake files (though doing so may halt an active government application).
