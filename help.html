Preparation Hub

Successful applications start with correct documents. Review the checklists below to avoid rejection.

International Renewals
Timeline: 3-5 Weeks Logistics: Courier to UK

Required Checklist:

  • Current Passport: Must be in hand (We ship this to HMPO in the UK).
  • Digital Photo (Special Rules):
    • Cream or Light Grey background (NOT White).
    • NO Glasses.
    • Neutral expression (No smiling).
    • Soft Copy Only: Do not scan a printed photo.
  • Dual Citizenship: If you have a Jamaican passport, provide the Number & Issue Date.
Timeline: 3-4 Weeks Logistics: Courier to Embassy

Required Checklist:

  • Current Passport: Must be submitted with the application.
  • 2x2 Inch Photo: One (1) physical photo. White background. No glasses.
  • DS-82 Form: We will fill this out for you digitally and print it for your signature.
  • Fee Payment: Payable via Pay.gov (Credit Card). We generate the receipt for the packet.
Timeline: 20 Business Days + Shipping Logistics: Mail to Canada

Required Checklist:

  • Current Passport: Must be submitted (and cannot be damaged).
  • 2 Physical Photos (Critical):

    Must be taken by a commercial photographer.

    • Size: 50 mm wide X 70 mm high.
    • Back of Photo: Must have the Studio Name, Address, and Date Stamped.
    • Neutral expression.
  • References: Names/Addresses of 2 people (not relatives) who have known you for 2+ years.
Local Services
Timeline: 7-10 Working Days

Checklist:

  • Old Passport: A clear photo or scan of the Bio-Data page.
  • Digital Photo: White background. No glasses. Digital file from studio.
  • 2 Emergency Contacts: Name, Address, Phone, Email.
Timeline: 7 Days (Express) vs 6 Weeks (Ordinary)

Required Info:

  • Entry Number: from Pink Slip. If missing, we must do a Search first.
  • Mother's Maiden Name: Critical for search.
Timeline: Same Day Processing

Required Documents:

  • Registration: White Slip or Title copy.
  • Insurance: Valid for 30+ days.
  • Fitness: Must be current.
