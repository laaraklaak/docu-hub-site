Preparation Hub

To ensure your application is approved quickly, please gather these documents before we meet.

Required Documents:

  • Old Passport: A clear photo or scan of the Bio-Data page (the page with your face).
  • Digital Photo: A "Soft Copy" file from a photo studio.
    Note: Do not scan a printed photo. It will fail.
  • Emergency Contacts: Name, Address, and Phone # for two (2) people.
  • Marriage Cert: Only required if your name has changed since your last passport.

Required Information:

  • Entry Number: E.g., "L12345". Usually found on the old "Pink Slip" or a previous copy.
  • Mother's Maiden Name: Her name before she was married. This is critical for the search.
  • Valid ID: We need a copy of your ID (Driver's License or Passport) to act as your agent.

Required Documents:

  • Original Registration: The old "White Slip" or a copy of the Title.
  • Insurance Cover Note: Must be valid and not expiring within 30 days.
  • Fitness Certificate: Must be current. (If expired, we can pay the fee, but you must take the car to the depot).
  • Authorization Letter: We will provide a letter for you to sign authorizing us to transact on your behalf.
