Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 2026
Effective for all visitors within Jamaica and international applicants.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit our website. They act as a short-term memory for the site, allowing us to recognize your device and store small amounts of information to make the site work efficiently.

Concierge Note: We do not use cookies to spy on you or access files on your device. They are purely functional tools to ensure your forms and service requests transmit securely.

2. How We Use Them

We categorize our cookies into two specific types:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are essential for the website to function. They allow you to navigate the site, open the service modals, and securely sign the digital intake forms. Without these, the "Concierge Protocol" services cannot be provided.

Performance & Analytics

We may use anonymous tools (such as Google Analytics) to count visits and traffic sources. This helps us understand which services (e.g., Passport vs. Driver's License) are most in demand, allowing us to allocate bearer resources effectively. This data is aggregated and anonymous.

3. No Third-Party Sales

Docu-Hub adheres to a strict privacy code. We do not sell, trade, or rent your cookie data to third-party advertisers. Your digital footprint on this site is used solely to facilitate your service request.

4. Managing Your Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may worsen your overall user experience, and the Intake Forms may not submit correctly.
