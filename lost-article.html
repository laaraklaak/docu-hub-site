Lost Your Jamaican Passport Overseas? Do This First.

Docu-Hub Advisory • 3 Min Read

Losing a passport while living or traveling overseas is an incredibly stressful experience. Whether it slipped out of your bag on the subway or was stolen along with your wallet, your absolute first priority must be protecting your identity.

The Passport, Immigration and Citizenship Agency (PICA) treats a lost passport as a serious, high-priority security issue. Because of this, you cannot simply log onto the portal and apply for a standard renewal. You must follow a strict legal protocol to ensure the missing passport is flagged globally before a replacement can be issued.

Step 1: The Mandatory Police Report

The very first thing you must do—before you even attempt to print out a new application—is file a formal report with your local police precinct in your current country of residence (US, UK, Canada, etc.).

Why is this required?

PICA requires an official Police Report to legally verify that the document is truly lost or stolen. This report allows them to invalidate the electronic chip inside the book and flag the passport number with INTERPOL, ensuring no one can use your identity to travel illegally.

Step 2: Securing Your Application Materials

Because you no longer have your previous passport to prove your identity, replacing a lost book requires much more rigorous documentation than a standard, physical renewal. You will need to gather:

  • 01

    The PICA Application Form

    You must completely fill out a new application form, paying special attention to the section that asks you to detail exactly how, when, and where the passport was lost.

  • 02

    Proof of Identity

    You must submit your Original Jamaican Birth Certificate, along with a valid, government-issued photo ID from your current country of residence (such as a Driver's License or State ID).

  • 03

    Penalty Fees

    Be prepared that PICA charges a significantly higher penalty fee to replace a lost or stolen passport compared to a standard renewal.

Step 3: Filing the Proxy Application

Because the online portal cannot process lost passports, the application, the photos, and the original police report must be physically submitted to a PICA agent. However, you do not need to book an emergency flight to Kingston to do this.
