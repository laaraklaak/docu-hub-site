Helpful Reference Info

Specific requirements by passport category.

Category 1: Adult Passport Renewals

For adults renewing an existing 10-year Jamaican passport:

  • Current / Expired Passport booklet.
  • Two (2) recent passport-sized photographs (one certified by an eligible official).
  • Tax Registration Number (TRN) card or number.
  • Completed PICA Passport Application Form.
Category 2: First-Time Adult & Minor (Child) Passports

For individuals obtaining a passport for the first time or for children under 18:

  • Official RGD Birth Certificate (with adoption or citizenship documents if applicable).
  • Parent or Legal Guardian's valid Photo ID (for minor applications).
  • Parent's consent signature on the application form.
  • Two (2) certified passport-sized photographs.
Category 3: Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Passports

If your previous passport was lost, stolen, or damaged, additional security verifications apply:

  • Official Police Report detailing the loss or theft.
  • Damaged passport booklet (if damaged).
  • Valid government-issued Photo ID (Voter's Card, Driver's Licence).
  • Completed PICA Lost/Damaged Passport Declaration Form.
Category 4: Name Changes & Marriage Adjustments

For passports requiring name updates following marriage, divorce, or deed poll:

  • Original RGD Marriage Certificate, Divorce Decree Absolute, or Deed Poll.
  • Current Passport booklet.
  • Updated TRN record matching your new legal name.
  • Two (2) certified passport-sized photographs in your new legal name.
Can Docu-Hub assist clients living outside Jamaica (Diaspora)?

Yes. We assist overseas clients with document preparation, eligibility audits, and photo compliance checks before you submit to your nearest Jamaican Consulate or Embassy, helping you avoid long mail-back turnaround delays.
