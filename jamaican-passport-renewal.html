01

First-Time Adult Application (18+)

For adults who have never held a Jamaican passport or whose last book was issued as a minor.

Click to Open

Reality Check: Here is the hard truth: you cannot use a proxy for a first-time adult passport. PICA strictly requires you to show up in person (at their office or a Consulate) to capture your fingerprints and biometrics. The good news? This is the only time you ever have to do this. Once your identity is in the system, you can use our proxy drop-off or the online portal for the rest of your life.

1 Gather Your Documents

  • Blank Application Form: You can download and print this directly from the official PICA website.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate: Must be the physical original, not a copy or laminated.
  • Valid Government Photo ID: Original Voter's ID, Driver's Licence, or National ID. (Note: School IDs are not accepted).
  • Printed Passport Photos: Two (2) professional physical photos.
  • Marriage Certificate: Original document (only required if you are currently or have been married).

2 Complete the Application Form

  • A
    Your Personal Details: Full current legal details exactly matching your birth certificate.
  • B
    Marital Status: To be completed if you are or have been married.
  • F
    Emergency Contacts: Two (2) emergency contacts required.
CRITICAL STEP: Do not sign your signature at the end (Section E) yet! You must sign this directly in front of a Justice of the Peace (JP), Pastor, or Police Officer. The official will watch you sign, then they will sign and stamp their section (Section G) and officially sign the back of one of your printed photos.

3 Your Final PICA Submission Pack

Once signed and stamped, put these items together to take to the PICA office (or hand over to our Docu-Hub team to drop off for you):

  • Completed Application Form (Sections E & G signed/stamped)
  • 1 Certified Photo + 1 Regular Photo
  • Original Valid Photo ID
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Original Marriage Certificate (if applicable)
  • Payment Method (Visa/Mastercard/Debit/Cash)

02

Adult Renewal — 100% Online Website

For standard adult renewals (passports issued after Sept 2001 with NO name change).

Click to Open

Reality Check: Renewing online sounds like a breeze until you get to the photo upload. The PICA system is notoriously unforgiving. You can fill out the entire form perfectly, but if your digital photo has a slight shadow, the wrong background tint, or is off by a few pixels, the system will just reject you without explaining why.

1 What you need to prepare

You do not need to print any physical paper forms for this. Before going to the PICA website, gather these specific items:

  • Do you qualify?: Your current passport must have been issued after September 2001, and your legal name must not have changed.
  • Active Email Address: You must be able to log into this email immediately to access the website.
  • Current Passport Book: Keep it handy so you can type in your old passport number and issue date.
  • Birth Certificate Number: You will need your Original RGD Birth Certificate to locate and type in your birth entry/certifying number (usually located on line 3).
  • Digital Photo: A digital image file formatted exactly to PICA's strict background and size rules.
  • Your Personal Details: Have your exact date of birth, mother's maiden name, and current residential address ready for delivery/pickup options.
  • Two (2) References: The full names, professions, complete physical addresses, phone numbers, and emails for two contact persons.
  • Payment Method: A valid Visa or Mastercard to process the government fee online.

2 How the Website Works

The Online Maze:

Go to the PICA website. They will take you through a series of qualification questions. Once you pass, you will type in your personal data, upload your digital photo, choose your delivery method (Pickup or Courier to your current address), and enter your card details to finalize the payment.

3 Final Online Submission Checklist

  • Passed the qualification questions
  • Digital photo that meets their strict sizing rules uploaded
  • Payment Processed & PICA Receipt Saved

03

Adult Renewal — In-Person or Name Change

If you got married/divorced, or if you want us to drop it off for you.

Click to Open

Reality Check: When changing your name or submitting physical forms, getting a Justice of the Peace (JP) to sign isn't just a formality—it is where most applications fail. If the JP forgets to date the form, uses the wrong stamp, or if their signature on the photo doesn't perfectly match the form, PICA will reject your entire package right at the counter.

1 What you need to bring

  • Current Passport: Must be physically surrendered to PICA.
  • RGD Birth Certificate: Original physical document.
  • Printed Photos: Two (2) physical photos. (If Name Change, ONE must be stamped by a JP on the back).
  • Proof of Name Change (if applicable): Original Marriage Certificate, Final Divorce Papers, or Deed Poll.

2 Required Form Sections

  • A
    Your Personal Details: Full name and info. (Use your NEW legal name here if you are doing a Name Change).
  • B
    Marital Status: To be completed if you are or have been married.
  • D
    Recent Passport Details: Your current passport number and issue date.
  • F
    Emergency Contacts: Two (2) emergency contacts required.
  • G
    The JP's Stamp: REQUIRED ONLY IF you are processing a Name Change.
CRITICAL STEP: Do not sign your signature at the end (Section E) yet! You must sign this directly in front of a Justice of the Peace (JP). The JP will watch you sign, stamp their section (if applicable), and officially sign the back of one of your printed photos.

04

Adult Passport Replacement (Lost or Damaged)

For lost, stolen, torn, washed, or damaged passport replacements.

Click to Open

Reality Check: Nobody wants to spend their day at a police station, but for a lost passport, it is mandatory. The catch? If the police officer forgets to use the official station stamp on your report, PICA won't accept it. If your passport is damaged, you have to write a formal legal affidavit explaining exactly how it got damaged—and if the wording isn't right, you are sent back to the drawing board.

1 Gather Your Documents

  • Blank Application Form: Downloaded and printed from the PICA website.
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate: Must be the physical original.
  • Valid Government Photo ID: Original Voter's ID, Driver's Licence, or National ID.
  • Printed Passport Photos: Two (2) professional physical photos.
  • IF YOUR PASSPORT IS DAMAGED: You must have the physical damaged passport book (regardless of its condition) AND a written statement explaining exactly how the damage occurred.
  • IF YOUR PASSPORT IS LOST OR STOLEN: You must have an Original Police Report (officially signed and stamped by the station).

2 Complete the Application Form

  • A
    Your Personal Details: Standard applicant info.
  • B
    Marital Status: To be completed if you are or have been married.
  • D
    Recent Passport Details: Fill in the details of the lost/damaged book if known.
  • F
    Emergency Contacts: Two (2) contacts required.
CRITICAL STEP: Do not sign your signature at the end (Section E) yet! You must sign this directly in front of a Justice of the Peace (JP). The JP will watch you sign, stamp their section (Section G), and officially sign the back of one of your printed photos.

Note for Damaged Passports: While you are at the JP, you must also have them properly sign and stamp your written statement about the damage so it is accepted by PICA.

3 Your Final PICA Submission Pack

Take these items to the PICA office (or hand them to our Docu-Hub team to drop off for you):

  • Completed Application Form (Sections E & G signed/stamped)
  • 1 Certified Photo + 1 Regular Photo
  • Original RGD Birth Certificate
  • Original Valid Photo ID
  • Proof of what happened: The Damaged Book & Stamped Statement (If Damaged) OR The Police Report (If Lost).
  • Payment Method (Visa/Mastercard/Debit/Cash)
