Who We Are

Your Trusted Proxy on the Ground

We bridge the gap between you and the Jamaican government, providing independent administrative processing for the diaspora and busy locals.

Company Overview

Docu-Hub Operations was founded on a simple principle: managing Jamaican government bureaucracy shouldn't require a plane ticket, missed days at work, or endless frustration.

Headquartered in Linstead, Jamaica, we are a private, independent administrative proxy desk. We act as your authorized representative—your boots on the ground—to navigate the complex physical and digital requirements of agencies like the TAJ (Tax Administration Jamaica), PICA (Passports, Immigration & Citizenship), and the RGD (Registrar General's Department).

Whether you are a Jamaican citizen living abroad needing vital records, or a local professional who simply cannot afford to spend hours in line, we ensure your documents are filed, tracked, and secured with complete confidentiality.

How We Operate

Our Approach & Commitment

We handle sensitive personal data every single day. Our operational standards are built entirely around security, efficiency, and clear communication.

Strict Confidentiality

Your personal records, identification documents, and financial receipts are handled with the highest level of discretion. We do not share your data with third parties beyond the required government agencies.

End-to-End Tracking

No more guessing if your application was received. We monitor portal updates, intercept "Missing Info" errors, and provide you with clear status updates from submission to document collection.

Diaspora-Focused

Living in the US, UK, or Canada? We specialize in cross-border logistics, ensuring your finalized passports, marriage certificates, and licenses are securely retrieved and dispatched to you globally.
