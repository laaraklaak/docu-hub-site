Legal Status & Identity

Docu-Hub Operations Services is a registered entity operating within the jurisdiction of Jamaica. We function under a dual-division model: as an administrative logistics firm providing specialized support for governmental processes, and as a bespoke design studio curating premium printed keepsakes and memorials.

Non-Legal & Non-Government Disclaimer

In strict accordance with the Legal Profession Act, Docu-Hub Operations Services is not a law firm, and its agents are not attorneys-at-law. We do not engage in the practice of law, nor do we offer legal representation or statutory interpretation.

NOTICE: Docu-Hub Operations Services is an independent private entity. We are NOT a government agency or an arm of Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ), the Passport, Immigration and Citizenship Agency (PICA), or the Registrar General’s Department (RGD).

Our role is exclusively limited to clerical assistance, custom design curation, acting as an administrative liaison with civil registries, and executing document logistics on behalf of our clients.

Regulatory Oversight (DNFBP)

Regarding our administrative division, we are recognized as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP) under the Proceeds of Crime Act (POCA) and the Terrorism Prevention Act. We operate under the oversight of relevant competent authorities and maintain robust Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT) protocols when handling sensitive compliance requests.

Data Privacy & Protection

Pursuant to the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA), we serve as a data controller. Highly sensitive administrative documents (such as IDs and TRNs required for eMVRC renewals) are stored in secure silos strictly for the fulfillment of government applications and are purged following service completion. Personal family data utilized for Keepsakes and Memorials is processed based entirely on voluntary client authorization for design purposes. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details.
