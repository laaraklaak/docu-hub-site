Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how our administrative proxy service works, our pricing structure, and how we protect your data.

Are you affiliated with PICA, RGD, or TAJ?

No. Docu-Hub Operations Services is a private, independent administrative proxy registered with the Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ). We are not a government agency. We act as your personal representative to navigate these government agencies on your behalf.

Do you only help Jamaicans living overseas?

Absolutely not. While we are a trusted proxy for the diaspora, a large part of our service is dedicated to local Jamaican professionals. We process online payments and stand in the physical lines so you don't have to burn your vacation days or miss a day of work.

Is it safe to send my original documents to you?

Yes. We follow strict data protection standards in accordance with Jamaica's Data Protection Act. For our overseas clients, we use secure, tracked international couriers for all document handling. We handle all physical documents with strict confidentiality and return them directly to you once the agency process is complete.

How much do your proxy services cost?

Because every document issue is unique (for example, generating a digital TAJ receipt versus legally correcting a misspelled name at RGD), we do not use one-size-fits-all pricing.

Once you message us with your specific issue, we assess the complexity and provide a clear, flat-fee quote before any work begins. There are no hidden fees.

What if my application gets rejected by the agency?

To prevent rejections, we perform a strict pre-audit of all your forms, signatures, and supporting documents before we ever submit them to the agency.

If an agency requires additional proof or historical records during the process, we contact you immediately to gather the necessary items and handle the follow-up lodgments on your behalf.

How do I start the process?

It is as simple as sending a WhatsApp message or booking a consultation on our calendar. You don't have to fill out long forms on our website. Just let us know what you need help with, and we will guide you through the exact next steps.
