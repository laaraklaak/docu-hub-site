General Service Protocols

Is it legal to use a third party for government documents?
Absolutely. Docu-Hub operates as an authorized bearer agency. For sensitive transactions (like Passport pickup or Tax Office transactions), we require a signed letter of authorization (which we provide) and your ID to act as your legal proxy.
How securely is my TRN and ID handled?
We adhere to the Jamaica Data Protection Act (2020). Your physical documents travel in lock-bags, and digital copies are stored in encrypted cloud nodes that automatically purge after 30 days. We do not sell data.
What areas do you cover?
Our primary bearer routes cover St. Catherine (Portmore/Spanish Town) and the Kingston Corporate Area. Requests outside these zones (e.g., Clarendon, St. Ann) attract a premium distance fee or are handled via courier partnership (Knutsford Express).

Traffic & Licensing (TAJ)

Why is my address update so critical?
Many drivers have warrants they don't know about because traffic tickets are mailed to old addresses. When we renew your license, we ensure your current address is updated in the TAJ system to prevent future legal surprises.
What documents do I need for Fitness Renewal?
1. Original Registration Certificate (Title not required).
2. Valid Insurance Cover Note.
3. The Vehicle itself (must be present at the depot).
Note: We offer a "Jockey Service" where a driver takes your car through the depot for you.
Can you pay my traffic tickets?
Yes. We perform a query on your TRN/License plate, generate a printout of outstanding fines, collect the funds from you, and clear them at the Tax Office, providing you with the official government receipt.

PICA & Identity

Can you renew a lost passport?
A lost passport requires a specific Lost/Stolen Report from the police and a statutory declaration. We can guide you through drafting the declaration and obtaining the police report before submitting the replacement application to PICA.
Why was my photo rejected by PICA?
PICA is strict. Common rejections include: wearing white shirts against a white background, heavy jewelry, or glasses. As part of our service, we review your photos before submission to ensure they meet the biometric standards.
